# Calendar Plugin

A per-campaign calendar, fantasy or real-world: custom months, weekdays,
moons, seasons, eras, events and day weather. V5 is a rebuild (#741) landing
in parts; this file describes what has shipped.

**Tier:** plugin (domain, repositories, service, handler, Templ page), shaped
like `internal/plugins/maps/`.

**Front end.** `static/css/calendar-view.css` (page, scoped `.cal-v5`);
`static/js/widgets/calendar_view.js` (`data-widget="calendar_view"`: grid,
cards, moons); `calendar_editor.js` (only with `data-can-edit="true"`;
augments the mounted view, never mounts alone); `calendar_event_drawer.js`
and `calendar_rule.js` (event editor and its pure rule logic);
`calendar_era_blend.js`, `calendar_era_look.js`; `calendar_weather_sheet.js`;
`calendar_sky_rail.js`; `calendar_game_night.js`; `chronicle_gen.js` (the
generator engine). `static/css/calendar_v5.css` styles the Calendars page,
the Era look part and the wizard; `static/js/calendar_open.js` runs the
cards' peek and opening.

**Moon phases.** Names come from `Moon.MoonPhaseName`, each centred on its
turning point. `calendar_view.js`, `sky_pane.js` and `chronicle_gen.js` keep
copies, held in step by `test/js/moon_phase_names.test.mjs`. Every phase is
read on the weekday counter (`Calendar.absDayIndex`, `CalDate.dayIndex`,
`SkyWorld.dayIndex`): the real Moon is anchored to the Julian Day Number,
which `AbsoluteDay` drifts from by a day each leap year. The lit shape is
`MoonLitPath`, mirrored by `MoonMath.litPath`.

## The page

The handler loads the calendar (`GetCalendarForViewer`) and the current
month's events (`ListEventsForMonth`) and renders a thin shell: a mount div
with viewer flags (`data-can-edit`, `data-can-author-dm-only`, `data-role`,
`data-api-base`) and a hidden JSON config, so first paint needs no fetch.
Everything else is client-side. The day and era cards' fold (`foldGeo`,
`foldAt`, `foldRun`) is ported from the signed mockup, since its timing is
the contract.

- **Grid:** weeks of the calendar's own length (`--cols`); intercalary
  months as a full-width band; a phase-accurate moon disc per day, or
  nested rings (at most three) when there are several moons.
- **Events:** a glyph per event (own icon, else kind's, else a dot), "+N"
  when busy, a glance card on hover, a day card on click, and a detail
  overlay, read-only for players.
- **Day and era cards** fold out as two leaves; a second press closes;
  phones get a sheet; reduced motion cross-fades. `refreshWing()` redraws
  an open card, never under a form being filled in.
- **Sky** (`SkyPane.Dock`, see `internal/widgets/sky/.ai.md`): today's sky
  at the top, folded by the **Sky** chip, remembered per viewer. Built from
  data this viewer already has, so no hidden moon or `dm_only` sky event
  shows. DM access adds a **Time** bar for privately looking at another
  moment; only **Set time** (owners, never on real-time calendars) writes,
  through the same PUT as the date form.
- **Moons:** a moon view with a per-moon strip and day ticks; moon-night
  payloads (`MoonNightPayload`) badge their span. Eclipse and harvest
  nights are not marked on the grid, by decision on #741. Owner-set moons
  and moon look: see below.
- **Event drawer** (`calendar_event_drawer.js`): every stored field.
  Untouched notes are not sent, so rich text written elsewhere survives.
  Delete is Owner-only, like its route.
- **Repeat by a rule** in the drawer: presets or "Make your own…", up to 6
  conditions, "Next five" from `POST .../recurrence/preview` (the browser
  never evaluates a rule). Conditions the rows can't edit (`months` with
  several, `season`, `after_event`) are shown as a sentence and sent back
  unchanged. The event picker reads `GET .../events/index?q=` (Player+;
  `{data:[{id,name,year,month,day,anchorable,phaseOnly}],truncated}`, ≤500).
  Save sends `recurrence_rule` as a rule, `null` (back to plain), or
  nothing (kept, including a rule the server hid from this viewer).
- **Repeating dates:** read from each event's `occurrences` in the month
  read; skipped dates are absent for players, struck through for editors.
  With `occurrences_truncated`, only what was found shows. Owners and
  co-Directors get "Move this one", "Skip this one" and "Undo"; the
  override key is the series date (`moved_from` for a moved one).
- **The deck** (editors): Look or Edit. Look hides writes in CSS; the DM
  team can "See it as players do" (`setPlayersView` re-reads with
  `?audience=players`, read as an anonymous Player, GET only). Never on
  while editing.
- **Edit mode:** select days by click, drag, weekday or week handles,
  keyboard or long-press. Bulk Hide/Reveal (`CanAuthorDmOnly`) and Shift
  events (a PUT per event); rule events and those with overrides are left
  alone and reported.
- **Keyboard:** arrows, Enter/Space, `T` today, Page Up/Down month
  (Shift: year). Escape closes one panel at a time; focus returns to the
  opener.

### Moons set by hand

`phase_mode` is `cycle` (`cycle_days`, `phase_offset`) or `manual`: the
moon holds its latest mark's state (`full`, `half`, `crescent`, `dark`,
`vanished`) until the next. `state_names` holds the Director's words. Marks
live in `calendar_moon_marks`, one per moon and day, each with `shown`; a
player sees only shown marks (`filterMoonsForViewer`). Writes:
`PUT .../moons/:moonID/mode`, `PUT`/`DELETE .../moons/:moonID/marks`
(`CanAuthorDmOnly`), and the AI page's `kind: moon` block. The real-world
Moon cannot be set by hand. Campaign export carries the mode, words and
marks; import matches the moon by name (`BackupService.RestoreMoonHand`).
Campaign export also carries each event's roll and stored nights, locks and
"tell players" included; a table roll's table is matched by name and the
roll is left off, and reported, when the campaign has no such table.

### Sun and moon colour strength

`Calendar.Sun` (`sun_look.go`): `red`, `orange`, `yellow` (default),
`white` or `blue`, with an optional exact `#rrggbb`. A moon's strength is its
`tint` column, 0–1 (`MoonStrength`, default 0.4). The owner sets both in the
**sky menu** (`calendar_sky_rail.js`, loaded only for the owner via
`data-sky-rail-src`) through `PUT .../sun` and `PUT .../moons/:moonID/look`
(Owner only). The AI page, calendar files and backups write them through
`SaveSunLook` and `SetMoonStrength`. Tests: `sun_look_test.go`,
`access_test.go`, `test/js/calendar_sky_rail_browser.test.mjs`.

## The Calendars page

Each card is a plain link to its calendar, so without script it is ordinary
navigation.

- **Peek:** hover or focus slides out a server-rendered month
  (`calendarPeek`), `aria-hidden`. On touch, first tap peeks, second opens.
- **Opening** (`calendar_open.js`): fetches the calendar page, mounts its
  `#main-content` in place (`Chronicle.mountWidgets`, `htmx.process`) and
  pushes the URL. Motes fly from the card to the calendar's pieces, about a
  second; the calendar is usable throughout. A failed fetch navigates.
- **Back:** the link, browser Back or Escape restores the kept list.
  Escape yields first to anything of the calendar's own.
- Reduced motion: 160ms crossfade. Any htmx swap of `#main-content` drops
  the kept list. Stylesheet links never move with the lifted page (a moved
  link reloads and flashes).
- Pure helpers: `test/js/calendar_open.test.mjs`.

## Eras

An era has a name, a start, an optional end (a year alone means the whole
year), a description, `color`, `color_2`, a blend `style` (`gas`/`ink`), an
optional `feel`, a lore page (`lore_entity_id`, same campaign, `ON DELETE
SET NULL`), a `dm_note` and `hidden_until_begins`. The calendar's look is
`Calendar.EraLook`: `era_colors_on`, `era_feel`, `era_intensity` (0.5–3),
`era_speed` (0–2.5). Years never restart.

**Visibility (`finishCalendarForViewer`).** Owner and co-Directors
(`SkipsPerUserRules`) get everything. Everyone else, Scribes included, gets
`erasForPlayer`: a still-hidden era (`EraIsSecret`) is left out, the era
before it reads as carrying on, and every `dm_note` is dropped. Lore pages
the viewer can't see are blanked (`redactEraLore`, failing closed). Events
inside a hidden era are dropped from every non-author read and from AI
export in Safe mode; the secret era's events route is 404. A Scribe dating
an event into a hidden era gets the neutral 422 "That date can't be used."
(`refuseSecretEraDate`); the sync API is judged the same way. Tests:
`era_secret_test.go`, `event_secret_era_write_test.go`.

**Not yet knowable.** `hideFromPlayer` is the one place an event read drops
what a non-author can't know yet: a future event announced only on its day,
then an event in a hidden era. Every viewer read calls it (authors pass
through). `not_yet_knowable_test.go` runs every read against one fixture,
and fails if an exported read that returns events isn't in its table.

**On the calendar.** `calendar_era_blend.js` paints a faint tint behind the
days, curling at era seams. Weak devices get a coarser ~30fps canvas;
reduced motion a still wash; the loop stops when idle or hidden. The era
chip opens the era panel (ribbon, dates, lore, key events, the Director's
note). `EraMath` is the pure part: a day's era is the latest-starting one
that has begun and not explicitly ended.

**Writing.** The era manager (`CanAuthorDmOnly`) creates, edits and deletes
eras; an edit sends no colour or order. The look is the structure editor's
**Era look** part (Owner): `PUT .../era-look` takes the whole look and
partial per-era fields (absent keeps, `null` clears) in one transaction.
Calendar-format export carries every era field except the lore page;
campaign export carries it by entity slug.

## Moon lore pages

`calendar_moons.lore_entity_id` (same rules as an era's). Reads go through
`finishCalendarForViewer`: hidden moons dropped, unseeable pages blanked
(`redactMoonLore`). The structure editor's Moons rows have a picker; a row
sends `lore_entity_id` with `lore_set: true` only once touched
(`MoonInput.LoreSet`), so a save can't overwrite a page changed elsewhere.
Every named page is checked to be in the campaign (`checkMoonLore`).
`parseWizardImportJSON` strips moon pages; `parseImportJSON` keeps them.
`MoonsForEntity` lists moons whose lore page is an entity, with tonight's
phase; it does not gate the entity itself. Campaign export carries the page
by slug; calendar-format export leaves it out.

## Page versions by era

`entity_era_versions`: one row per page and era of the default calendar,
holding only what differs (NULL name, picture or text is the usual page's;
`fields_data` holds differing values, `null` clears). `entry_rev` detects
save clashes. `EraVersionService` reads eras through
`GetDefaultCalendarForViewer`, so era visibility applies; `EraIndexAt`
matches `EraMath.at`. Writes refuse another campaign's era or page as
NotFound; text goes through `sanitize.HTML`. Campaign export carries
versions by page slug (not the picture). Drawing and editing is the
entities plugin's ("Pages by era"), wired in
`internal/app/era_pages_adapter.go`.

## The Time line block

`page_timeline`: an entity page block (calendar add-on, one per page, "Page
events" in the Customizer), a hairline of beads for the page's dated events.
Spec: #1546.

- **Events:** from the default calendar (else the first) via
  `ListEventsForPage`: events whose `entity_id` is the page plus those in
  `entity_event_links`, each once, filtered exactly like a month read for
  the viewer. A repeating event sits on its first date. At most 200.
- **Setting:** `show_dm` (`show`/`hide`).
- **Death:** the latest event whose kind is a death (`isDeathKind`) ends
  the line.
- **Drawing:** the server sets `--ptl-lit`; `page_timeline.js` merges beads
  closer than a bead's width (`labelYears` does it server-side without
  script). Style kits draw it from `--kit-tl-*` (`style_kits.css`).
- **Links:** a bead opens its day (`view?event=<id>&date=…`). The link
  under the line goes to the first visible time line following the
  calendar (`pageTimelineLinker` in `internal/app/routes.go`, since this
  plugin may not read time lines), else to the calendar.
- **Empty:** a quiet line for the Director, nothing for a player.

## Day weather

`calendar_weather_days`: one reading per day, `source` `manual` or
`generated`, plus `locked`. A generated write never replaces a manual or
locked reading; a manual write clears the lock.

- `GET .../weather/days?year=[&month=]`: players get days up to today
  only; Owner and co-Directors get all, with `locked` (absent for players).
- `PUT .../weather/days`, `POST .../weather/days/clear`,
  `POST .../weather/days/lock` (`{"days":[…],"locked":bool}`, answers
  `{"changed":N}`): `CanAuthorDmOnly`, at most 1000 days, dates checked.
- `GET .../weather/forecast` (same gate, also public): `[]` unless
  `calendars.forecasts_enabled`; else one entry per lead day with a stored
  reading, blurred by `weather_forecast.go` with draws seeded by an HMAC
  under a key derived from `SECRET_KEY` (`SetForecastSecret`), so the blur is
  stable but a player cannot replay it. Only icon,
  words and numbers are sent, never the real preset, label, colour,
  description or wind, so a hidden day can't leak.
- `GET .../weather/settings` (`CanAuthorDmOnly`): climate, duration,
  `kinds`, `forecast_days` (1–10, default 5), `forecasts_enabled`.
  `calendar_weather_settings.kinds` is validated by `weather_kinds.go`
  (mirrored by `validateWeatherKind` in `chronicle_gen.js`; a JS test pins
  them). In Calendar settings the kinds editor posts one hidden
  `weather_kinds` field (absent keeps, `[]` clears); the forecast checkbox
  travels with a `weather_forecast_sent` marker, since an unchecked box
  sends nothing.
- **The weather sheet** (`calendar_weather_sheet.js`): a month in the dock
  drawer where the Director picks days, paints, erases, pins (a pin is the
  lock) and rolls with `chronicle_gen.js` (loaded on first use from
  `data-engine-src`). Everything is a draft previewed on the calendar until
  Save; `CalendarEditor._saveWeather` writes clears, readings, then locks,
  in batches of 1000, with Undo.

Campaign export carries every day reading with its source and lock
(`BackupService`); calendar-format export leaves them out.

## Game nights and availability

All of this is the sessions plugin's data; the calendar stores nothing.
Shown only on a real-world calendar with the campaign's Game nights switch
on (`data-game-nights`).

- **Nights** from `GET /campaigns/:id/sessions/nights` (members): a dice
  mark on the day and RSVP in the day card (`/sessions/:sid/rsvp`,
  `/rsvp-exclude`). Times read in the calendar's zone (`data-zone`), with
  "Show in my time". `/game-nights` links open with `?night=…&date=…`.
- **Who's free** reads `GET /campaigns/:id/availability/overlay?week=&tz=`,
  which decides who sees names. DM access gets Best times and per-player
  lines; players get their own week (`GET /availability/mine`). Day cards
  offer "Change this day" (`PUT /availability/exceptions`).
- **The planner** (dock drawer): "Everyone" for DM access, "My hours" for
  all, weekly or alternating (`PUT /availability/mine`, cadence counted by
  `WeekCadenceFor`).
- **Planning** posts the New Session form's fields to
  `POST /campaigns/:id/sessions` with `HX-Request` (204, no redirect). The
  game night editor (`calendar_game_night.js`, Scribe+) sends only changed
  fields to `PUT /sessions/:sid`; Owner and co-Directors can cancel.
- **A night's page:** recap and linked pages from `GET /sessions/:sid/page`;
  recap via `PUT /sessions/:sid/recap` (formatted recaps are read-only
  here so a save can't drop formatting); links via `POST`/`DELETE
  /sessions/:sid/entities`.

## New-calendar wizard

`GET /campaigns/:id/calendars/wizard` (Owner): an HTMX dialog swapped into
`#calv5-wizard-root`. Sources: Real-world, a preset, Build your own, import,
Generate one. Once anything changes, closing asks first ("Not saved yet").

**Every source becomes an `ImportResult` and goes through
`CreateCalendarFromImport`**, so every one is re-validated
(`checkCalendarImportLimits`, `clampCalendarStructure`, names, colours).
Browser-submitted JSON is re-parsed by `parseWizardImportJSON`, never
trusted because it round-tripped a hidden field.

- **Real-world** (`reallife.go`): Gregorian months (4/100/400 leap rule),
  weekdays Monday first (`gregorianJDN % 7`), the real Moon from a mean
  synodic month (can drift up to half a day; warned), and four
  northern-hemisphere seasons (a guess; warned). With "today follows the
  real date" on, `WizardCreate` computes today from the server clock in the
  chosen zone, never from the browser.
- **Build your own:** an Alpine editor serialized to the import JSON shape.
- **Generate one:** client-side over `chronicle_gen.js` (its header
  documents `ChronicleGen.*`); the server never runs it. Parts can be kept
  or rerolled. `htmx.config.allowScriptTags=false` strips any swapped
  `<script>`, so the step loads the ~460KB engine itself
  (`ensureEngine()`, `document.createElement('script')`). "Work it out from
  the sky" uses `ChronicleGen.calendar.fromSky` (tests:
  `test/js/chronicle_gen.test.mjs`). Review posts `generated_json`;
  `parseGeneratedCalendarJSON` caps its size and runs the same
  `PreviewImport` parser and clamps as an upload. This route is not in
  `app.go`'s `calendarImportPathPattern`, so it keeps the 2MB body limit.

## Edit structure

`GET .../calendars/:calid/structure` (Owner) is the wizard's editor
prefilled from the calendar (`calendarEditorState`), plus seasons and
Festival/Rest day flags, minus eras. Real-time calendars get an explanation
instead.

1. **Preview** (`POST .../structure/preview`): re-validated like a wizard
   build, then `PreviewStructureEdit` plans without writing. Any edit
   empties the preview.
2. **Save** (`POST .../structure`) with the preview's fingerprint:
   `ApplyStructure` locks the calendar row (`FOR UPDATE`), re-plans, and
   writes nothing if the fingerprint changed (the fresh preview replaces
   the old). Otherwise one transaction writes everything.

`planStructureEdit` rules:
- **Months match by name**; a new name in an old place is a rename.
- **Moved:** events (and end and repeat-until months), eras, day weather,
  rule `month`/`months` conditions and overrides follow their month
  (`remapMonthPositions`, `remapWeatherDays`, `remapOverrides`; swap-safe).
- **Re-dated:** an event in a month with no counterpart is untouched and
  shows in whatever month now holds its position; named in the preview.
- **Stranded:** a date that will no longer exist is kept and named.
- A moved weather reading or override wins a clash and the other is
  deleted; the preview says so. Rules naming a removed month, moon or
  season are left as written and named.
- **Today** follows its month, clamped only if it would be gone.
- **Moons and seasons** keep their ids, so hidden flag, colour, offset,
  look, lore page and phase rows survive.

Not editable here (a save keeps them): a moon's phase offset, colour and
look; a season's colour, description and weather effect. Sessions dated
in-world are not re-dated (the sessions plugin owns them).

## Repeat rules and overrides

`recurrence_type`: `weekly`, `biweekly`, `custom` (every
`recurrence_interval` weeks), `monthly`, `yearly`, or `rule`, which carries:

```json
{"match": [{"kind": "moon_phase", "moon_id": 3, "phase": "full"},
           {"kind": "weekday", "weekday": 5}],
 "every": 1, "offset_days": 0}
```

A day matches when every condition holds; `every: N` keeps every Nth match
from the start date; `offset_days` (−365..365) shifts each.
`recurrence_max_occurrences` and `recurrence_end_*` still bound it. 1–6
conditions:

| `kind` | Fields | Matches |
|---|---|---|
| `moon_phase` | `moon_id`, `phase` (`new`, `first_quarter`, `full`, `last_quarter`) | the day the grid shows that phase |
| `weekday` | `weekday` or `weekdays` (0-based) | that weekday; intercalary days have none |
| `nth_weekday` | `n` (1–5, −1 last), `weekday` | nth/last such weekday of its month |
| `day_of_month` | `day` (1-based, −1 last) | that day of any month |
| `month` / `months` | 1-based | any day of those months |
| `season` / `season_start` | `season_id` (optional for `season_start`) | the season / its first day (any season's, without an id) |
| `after_event` | `event_id`, `days` (−365..365) | `days` after each occurrence of another event |
| `relative_to_event` | `event_id` | the same days as another event |

Dates use `Calendar.absDayIndex`. Referenced ids must belong to this
calendar and be visible to the writer. An anchor can't itself be relative
to another event (no chains); a hidden dependent never blocks a Scribe's
save, and expansion follows one level only. Parsed strictly (unknown
fields, >4 KB, trailing data refused); partial-update field (absent keeps,
`null` clears); stored only on `rule` events.

**Expansion.** `ListEventsForMonth` fills `occurrences`
(`[{year,month,day,skipped?,moved_from?}]`). Uncounted rules scan at most
five years; counted ones walk from the start, up to 1000 years. One request
tests at most 1,500,000 days. An event that hits a bound comes back with
`"occurrences_truncated": true`: its dates are unknown, not absent. Other
reads (upcoming, timeline, search, single event) are not expanded.

**Overrides.** `calendar_event_overrides`, keyed by the series date: `skip`
or `move`. Skips show flagged to Scribe and up, absent for players;
`moved_from` is editors-only. Deleted with the event.

**API.** Events take `recurrence_rule`.
`PUT .../events/:eid/occurrences/:y/:m/:d` with `{"action":"skip"}` or
`{"action":"move",…}`, and `DELETE` (undo), need edit rights; the date must
be a real occurrence and a move onto another occurrence conflicts.
`POST .../recurrence/preview` returns `{"dates":[…],"truncated":bool}` (≤10)
for any reader.

**Visibility.** A rule naming a hidden moon or event is omitted for that
viewer; the dates stay. The Foundry module does not read `occurrences` yet.

**Export.** Campaign export carries rules and overrides; import creates
anchors first, rewrites ids, then replays overrides (skips, then moves).
Calendar-format export carries rules but not overrides; an unusable rule
on import becomes a one-off on its start date, with a warning.

## An event's linked pages

- `GET .../events/:eid/pages` (Player+), after `GetEventForViewer`'s gates;
  `EntitiesForEvent` filters by entity visibility.
- `POST .../events/:eid/pages` `{"entity_id"}`,
  `DELETE .../events/:eid/pages/:entityID` (Scribe+, event visible to the
  caller). Same campaign only; role `involved`.

Separate from the event's single `entity_id` ("Linked page").

## Data model

`model.go`; schema overview in `.ai/data-model.md`. `GetCalendarForViewer`
eager-loads `Months`, `Weekdays`, `Moons`, `Seasons`, `Eras`, `EventKinds`
(campaign-scoped, shared by every calendar), `Cycles`, `Festivals`,
`Weather`. Non-authors get `EventKinds` stripped and moons and eras
filtered (ADR-049).

A campaign with calendars always has one marked default (`is_default`): its
first calendar is created default, deleting the default hands the mark to
the next by sort order, and the boot reconciler `ReconcileDefaultCalendars`
marks the first calendar in any campaign left without one. Readers that can
only follow one calendar and must still work mid-repair (the DM Screen, the
quest and game-night anchors) use `GetPrimaryCalendarForViewer`.

## Security

- Every read is viewer-filtered (`permissions.Viewer`, ADR-049). Linked
  entities a viewer can't see are blanked (`redactHiddenEntityLinks`,
  failing closed).
- Hidden eras and Director's notes never reach a player.

## Dependencies

- **Uses:** campaigns (access, `CanAuthorDmOnly`), auth (optional
  `GetUser` for the wizard's zone, `Handler.SetTimezoneLookup`), addons
  (`calendar` gate), entities (visibility gate, links), sessions (game
  nights and availability, over HTTP from the page).
- **Publishes:** through `CalendarEventPublisher`;
  `internal/app/calendar_event_publisher.go` maps to WebSocket types, all
  `RequiresDM` (the payload is the unfiltered row).
- **Used by:** `syncapi` (through `CalendarService`); the Foundry calendar
  window through the notes grant (`registerCalendarJSONRoutes`, one list
  for both, plus `GET /calendars/embed`).
