# Trackers Plugin

## Purpose

The Trackers page block (`trackers`, one per page): numbers, named steps and
clocks a Director keeps on a page, each with a dated log. A tracker is a *kind*
(shape and rules, saved once per campaign) placed on a page. Registered in
`internal/app/routes.go` behind the plugin's health gate; works on any page type.
A layout places the block; a page a game system draws has no layout, so the block
also offers itself as a tab of that page's panel (`BlockMeta.PageTab`, filled in
`internal/app/trackers_adapters.go`): first when the viewer can see a tracker
there, after Items when they can only add one, absent otherwise.

## Shapes

| Shape | Value runs | Shown as |
|---|---|---|
| `count` | `min`..`max`; either end may be open | the number, and bands as a lit bar |
| `steps` | 0..steps-1 (2 to 20 named steps) | the steps as chips, the current one in the accent |
| `clock` | 0..segments (2 to 24) | a segmented ring |

Bands (`count` and `clock`) say what a threshold means: the band in force is
the highest whose `at` is at or below the value, and its words are the
tracker's "now" line. A step's own meaning is the "now" line for `steps`.

## The ledger

A tracker stores no value. Every change is a line in `tracker_entries`
(`add n` or `set n`), and the value is the fold of the unstruck lines from the
kind's start value, clamped to the kind's ends after each line (`ledger.go`).
A wrong line is struck, not deleted: it stays in the log crossed out and no
longer counts, and can be brought back. Each line carries the in-game date of
the campaign's default calendar when it was written (`GameClock`); with no
calendar it is undated, never refused.

## Who may do what

| | Read | Log a change (add, set) | Strike, place, rename, hide, remove | Kinds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Player, Scribe, public viewer | trackers shown to everyone, no notes | no | no | no |
| Chosen player, "may look" | trackers shown to everyone, and the ones that name them; their own notes | no | no | no |
| Chosen player, "may change it" | as above | yes, on a placed tracker that names them | no | no |
| Director / granted co-Director | everything | yes | yes | yes |

"Director" is `CampaignContext.CanAuthorDmOnly()`. The service refuses every
write for anyone else, whatever route reached it. Director-only data is
stripped on the server: hidden trackers are not sent, log notes are left out
of a player's view (a player keeps their own lines' notes), and the kinds list
is sent to Directors only. A page the viewer cannot open answers 404, exactly
like a missing one, and so does a tracker the viewer is not meant to see, on
every route that names it (a log, strike, rename or remove by a player who
cannot see it is a 404, never a 403). A player who sees a tracker but may not
do the thing gets 403.

## Chosen players

A tracker's visibility is `directors`, `everyone` or `players`. `players`
shows it to the Directors and to the users listed in `tracker_players`
(`user_id`, `can_change`); `sees()` in `service.go` is the one decision and
needs a current member (`Viewer.MemberRole` at least Player), so a former
member or a public-campaign visitor never matches a row left behind. The
repository fills `Tracker.Players` on every tracker read, so no caller can
decide visibility without the list. A tracker not shown to a viewer is not
sent, not counted, and never reaches the world's clocks block (which reads
only `everyone` trackers). A kind's own default and a newly placed tracker are
only `directors` or `everyone`: `players` needs a list, so it is set on a
placed tracker with PATCH. A virtual every-category tracker (no row yet)
cannot have chosen players until it is placed or changed once; its label in
the widget is plain text, not the panel's quiet word.

A grant counts only while its `created_at` (when the Director chose the
player) is not before the member's `joined_at`: a player removed and invited
back is not silently restored, and the Director's picker shows them unticked
until chosen again. `grantFilter` in `service.go` applies this wherever the
service reads trackers; a pick left unchanged by a later save keeps its
original time.

Only current members who are not Directors can be chosen (the service checks
against `MemberDirectory`; an unknown id or a Director is refused, and one bad
pick refuses the whole list). Switching a tracker away from `players` clears
its rows, so choosing `players` again starts from nobody and an old grant
never re-arms. A PATCH that carries no `players` key keeps the list; `players`
sent while the visibility is not `players` is refused.

A chosen player with `can_change` logs through the same route as a Director,
by `trackerId` only (a player cannot make a row from a kind), as an `add` or
`set` line with an optional note, counted at once; the change listener hears
it with that player as the viewer. Homebrew rules run for it with the
Director's standing (`internal/app/homebrew_rules.go`), since the recipe is the
Director's. Every hand line records its writer in `created_by`: a Director
sees a player's lines named ("16 Frostmoot · −3 · Tam · Fed on the stag"), the
player sees their own as "You" with their note, and rule lines and Directors'
own lines carry no name. A chosen player's copy carries `canChange` and
`alsoSeenBy` (the other chosen players' names), shown as one muted line.

## Kinds on every page of a category

A kind with `everyTypeId` appears on every page of that category without
being placed, as a virtual tracker (`id` empty). Its first change creates the
real row (`ensure`), so a category of a hundred pages costs nothing until one
is used. A kind already placed on a page, or a placed tracker with the same
name, takes its place.

## Kinds a recipe keeps

A Homebrew Workshop recipe's tracker and clock parts are saved as kinds with an `owner` (`homebrew:<recipe>:<kind>:<part key>`), through `CheckOwned` and
`SyncOwned` (`owned.go`), called from `internal/app/homebrew_adapters.go`.
Each sync is the whole picture for one recipe and kind of part: a kind is
created or updated in place (keeping its category), and one no longer wanted
is deleted when unused or let go when on pages, staying as a hand-made kind
with its trackers and logs. The sync refuses what the kinds' own rules would:
a bad shape, a name another kind has, two parts with one name, a shape change
under placed trackers, too many kinds. An owned kind shows `managed` to the
Director, can be placed like any other, and refuses a hand edit or delete:
it changes only through its recipe.

The **owned view** (`OwnedPage`, `?owner=<prefix>`, the widget's
`data-owner`) shows only the trackers of kinds owned under a prefix ending in
`:`, with a virtual tracker for each such kind not yet on the page, and
offers no kinds to add. It is how a placed recipe block (`homebrew_recipe`)
shows its trackers: the block on a page layout is their placement, so a
change sent with that `owner` creates the row on its first use, as an
every-page kind's does.

**Rules.** `SetChangeListener` (called once at start-up from
`internal/app/routes.go`) hears every change, log line or strike, that moves
a tracker, with the kind's owner ("" for a hand-made kind), its name, the
page and the value before and after; the homebrew rules run from there, for
the recipe that owns the kind or the recipe parts linked to it by name. The change is
saved first, so a listener's error is logged, not returned. A rule changes
trackers back through `LogOwned(campaign, page, owner, …)`: one log line,
source `rule`, on that page's tracker (created on first use) or, with no
page, on every page that has one. A recipe part linked to a hand-made kind
changes it through `LogKind(campaign, page, name, …)`, the same line, but
only where the kind already shows (placed, or on every page of the page's
category): placing a hand-made kind stays the Director's choice. `KindNamed`
finds a kind by name, ignoring case, for the Workshop's check of such a link.
`Pages` lists the pages that have a tracker, by name, for the Homebrew drawer.

**Change observer.** `SetChangeObserver` (called once at start-up from
`internal/app/routes.go`) is told, with only the campaign id, after any
saved change that a rule reading trackers may judge: a log line (`Log`,
`LogOwned`, a rule's), `Strike`, `Place`, `Patch`, `Remove`, `UpdateKind` and
`DeleteKind`, for every kind and not only owned ones. Unlike the listener it
carries nothing, so the observer must look again rather than trust the event,
and it must not block. Card decks use it to wake their watcher
(`TestChangeObserver`). A refused write tells it nothing.

## The world's clocks block

`GET /campaigns/:id/world-clocks` (public-capable, `world_clocks.go`): the
campaign home's `world_clocks` block, up to six trackers drawn small as a ring
(the value against its top, or just the number when unbounded), the name and
the "now" line. `Service.Shared` reads as a plain player for everyone, the owner
included: only placed trackers set to Everyone (never a virtual per-category
one), on pages a player may open, whatever the caller's role. A ring's fill is
an integer percent built Go-side. More than six leaves a muted "N more, on
their pages", since trackers have no page of their own. No trackers: nothing
for players and guests, a one-line hint for the owner. A guest gets it only
where the owner placed it where a guest may open it (the Guests home, or the
page named by `?on=`; `campaigns.BlockVisibleTo`).

## Routes (JSON)

| Method | Path | Who |
|--------|------|-----|
| GET | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/page/:eid` | anyone who can open the page (public campaigns included); `?owner=<prefix>` is the owned view. Directors also get each tracker's `players` and the page's `members` (the players that can be chosen); a chosen player gets `canChange` and `alsoSeenBy` |
| GET | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/pages` | Director: `[{id, name}]`, every page with a tracker, by name |
| POST | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/page/:eid` | Director: place a kind |
| POST | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/page/:eid/log` | Director: one change, by `trackerId` or `kindId`; a chosen player who may change it: by `trackerId` only |
| PATCH, DELETE | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/:tid` | Director: rename, or set `visibility` (`directors`, `everyone`, `players`) with `players: [{userId, canChange}]`; absent keys keep, `players: []` chooses nobody; remove with its log |
| POST | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/entries/:entry/strike` | Director: `{"struck": bool}` |
| GET, POST | `/campaigns/:id/tracker-kinds` | Director |
| PUT, DELETE | `/campaigns/:id/tracker-kinds/:kid` | Director; PUT needs `version`, a kind in use cannot change shape or be deleted, a `managed` kind cannot be changed here at all |

## Look

Theme tokens only, with the accent from `--annals-acc`, so a page look, a
style kit and the campaign colour all reach it. A Director-only tracker has
`data-dm-only` (the amber edge, hidden in the player preview) and its label
`cb-dm`; a chosen player's own copy has no amber edge. The visibility label is
a quiet word that opens the "Who sees this" panel under the row: three radios,
and for chosen players a tick per member with "May look" or "May change it",
every choice saved as it is made. A player who may change a tracker gets the
minus and plus (and the steps and clock controls) and a "Why, for the log" note
that goes with their next change. Controls follow `.ai/ui-standard.md`: quiet
words, 32px icons, one filled button per form.

## Scope

The block is placed with the Customizer like any other. Homebrew that combines
trackers with rules is a Homebrew Workshop recipe; that, the DM Screen drawer
and the party clock strip are tracked under #1569. AI Import makes kinds,
places them and logs changes through this service as the Director
(`internal/app/ai_trackers.go`); `CheckKind` lets its review refuse a bad kind
before commit.
