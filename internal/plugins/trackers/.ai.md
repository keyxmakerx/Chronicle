# Trackers Plugin

## Purpose

The **Trackers** page block (`trackers`, one per page): numbers, named steps
and clocks a Director keeps on a page, each with a dated log. A tracker is a
*kind* (the shape and its rules, saved once per campaign) placed on a page.
Uses: a need that rises, an ink count from 0 to 3, a power tally, a crafting
project, a clock of tavern bells. It is registered in `internal/app/routes.go`
behind the plugin's health gate and works on any page type.

## Shapes

| Shape | Value runs | Shown as |
|---|---|---|
| `count` | `min`..`max`; either end may be open | the number, and bands as a lit bar |
| `steps` | 0..steps-1 (2 to 20 named steps) | the steps as chips, the current one in the accent |
| `clock` | 0..segments (2 to 24) | a segmented ring |

Bands (`count` and `clock`) say what a threshold means: the band in force is
the highest whose `at` is at or below the value, and its words are the
tracker's "now" line. A step's own meaning is the "now" line for `steps`.

## The ledger

A tracker stores no value. Every change is a line in `tracker_entries`
(`add n` or `set n`), and the value is the fold of the unstruck lines from the
kind's start value, clamped to the kind's ends after each line (`ledger.go`).
A wrong line is struck, not deleted: it stays in the log crossed out and no
longer counts, and can be brought back. Each line carries the in-game date of
the campaign's default calendar when it was written (`GameClock`); with no
calendar it is undated, never refused.

## Who may do what

| | Read | Change, log, strike | Place, rename, hide, remove | Kinds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Player, Scribe, public viewer | trackers shown to everyone, no notes | no | no | no |
| Director / granted co-Director | everything | yes | yes | yes |

"Director" is `CampaignContext.CanAuthorDmOnly()`. The service refuses every
write for anyone else, whatever route reached it. Director-only data is
stripped on the server: hidden trackers are not sent, log notes are left out
of a player's view, and the kinds list is sent to Directors only. A page the
viewer cannot open answers 404, exactly like a missing one.

## Kinds on every page of a category

A kind with `everyTypeId` appears on every page of that category without
being placed, as a virtual tracker (`id` empty). Its first change creates the
real row (`ensure`), so a category of a hundred pages costs nothing until one
is used. A kind already placed on a page, or a placed tracker with the same
name, takes its place.

## Kinds a recipe keeps

A Homebrew Workshop recipe's tracker and clock parts are saved as kinds with
an `owner` (`homebrew:<recipe>:<kind>:<part key>`), through `CheckOwned` and
`SyncOwned` (`owned.go`), called from `internal/app/homebrew_adapters.go`.
Each sync is the whole picture for one recipe and kind of part: a kind is
created or updated in place (keeping its category), and one no longer wanted
is deleted when unused or let go when on pages, staying as a hand-made kind
with its trackers and logs. The sync refuses what the kinds' own rules would:
a bad shape, a name another kind has, two parts with one name, a shape change
under placed trackers, too many kinds. An owned kind shows `managed` to the
Director, can be placed like any other, and refuses a hand edit or delete:
it changes only through its recipe.

The **owned view** (`OwnedPage`, `?owner=<prefix>`, the widget's
`data-owner`) shows only the trackers of kinds owned under a prefix ending in
`:`, with a virtual tracker for each such kind not yet on the page, and
offers no kinds to add. It is how a placed recipe block (`homebrew_recipe`)
shows its trackers: the block on a page layout is their placement, so a
change sent with that `owner` creates the row on its first use, as an
every-page kind's does.

**Rules.** `SetChangeListener` (called once at start-up from
`internal/app/routes.go`) hears every Director's change, log line or strike,
that moves a tracker of an owned kind, with the kind's owner, the page and
the value before and after; the homebrew rules run from there. The change is
saved first, so a listener's error is logged, not returned. A rule changes
trackers back through `LogOwned(campaign, page, owner, …)`: one log line,
source `rule`, on that page's tracker (created on first use) or, with no
page, on every page that has one. `Pages` lists the pages that have a
tracker, by name, for the Homebrew drawer.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `embed.go`, `migrations/00*.sql` | slug `trackers`, block type, embedded schema (idempotent); `002` adds a kind's `owner` |
| `model.go` | shapes, `Viewer`, `Kind`, `Tracker`, `Entry`, views, inputs |

| `owned.go` | `OwnedKind`, `CheckOwned`, `SyncOwned`: kinds a recipe keeps in step |

| `repository.go` | hand-written SQL; every query is scoped by campaign |
| `service.go` | every rule about who sees and writes what |
| `handler.go`, `routes.go` | bind, call, respond; route gates |
| `block.templ` | the block's mount point |
| `static/js/trackers.js`, `static/css/trackers.css` | the widget and its look; pure helpers are on `window.TrackerHelpers` |

## Routes (JSON)

| Method | Path | Who |
|--------|------|-----|
| GET | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/page/:eid` | anyone who can open the page (public campaigns included); `?owner=<prefix>` is the owned view |
| GET | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/pages` | Director: `[{id, name}]`, every page with a tracker, by name |
| POST | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/page/:eid` | Director: place a kind |
| POST | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/page/:eid/log` | Director: one change, by `trackerId` or `kindId` |
| PATCH, DELETE | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/:tid` | Director: rename or hide; remove with its log |
| POST | `/campaigns/:id/trackers/entries/:entry/strike` | Director: `{"struck": bool}` |
| GET, POST | `/campaigns/:id/tracker-kinds` | Director |
| PUT, DELETE | `/campaigns/:id/tracker-kinds/:kid` | Director; PUT needs `version`, a kind in use cannot change shape or be deleted, a `managed` kind cannot be changed here at all |

## Look

Theme tokens only, with the accent from `--annals-acc`, so a page look, a
style kit and the campaign colour all reach it. A Director-only tracker has
`data-dm-only` (the amber edge, hidden in the player preview) and its label
`cb-dm`. Controls follow `.ai/ui-standard.md`: quiet words, 32px icons, one
filled button per form.

## Where it fits

The block is an everyday widget: it is placed on a page with the Customizer
like any other. Homebrew that combines trackers with rules (a need that rises
on its own, item use that steps a tracker) is a saved recipe built in the
Homebrew Workshop, which is a separate piece of work under #1569, as are the
DM Screen drawer, the party clock strip and AI Import.
