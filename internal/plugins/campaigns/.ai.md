# Campaigns Plugin

Core plugin, always enabled. A campaign is the top-level container for entities,
maps, timelines and the rest. This plugin owns the campaign pages and dashboards,
settings, members and roles, ownership transfer, the sidebar, the Customize
hub and campaign export/import.

## Domain Concepts

- **Roles:** Owner (3), Scribe (2), Player (1), None (0, a site admin who has not
  joined). Compare numerically (`>=`). Co-DM is the Scribe role plus a DM grant.
- **Dual permission model:** `MemberRole` for content visibility, `IsSiteAdmin`
  for admin actions.
- **Ownership transfer:** token flow, 72h expiry, optional email.
- **Hand-over when the owner is away:** the owner picks Never (default), 3, 6 or
  12 months (`handover_after_months`, owner-only `PUT /campaigns/:id/handover-setting`).
  Once `last_seen_at` is that far back, a site admin may give the campaign to an
  existing non-guest member (`AdminHandOver`, `handover.go`). Every rule is
  re-checked there, a pending transfer is cancelled, the old owner becomes Co-DM
  and is emailed.

## Foundry

- **Connect line.** The Foundry row on `/campaigns/:id/extensions` shows a status
  dot, module version and a paste box. It reads through `FoundryConnector`
  (`SetFoundryConnector`, wired in `internal/app/routes.go` by
  `foundryConnectorAdapter` over the sync key service, the websocket hub and
  `BaseURL`); a nil connector leaves the plain "Set up" link. Status is the hub's
  live socket, else the newer of the hub's last contact and the newest
  `last_used_at` of the campaign's active keys: under 24 hours green, longer
  amber, nothing grey. The paste box shows only the newest active key's stored
  prefix. "Make a new connect line" mints an additional key (read, write, sync)
  and shows the full line once (`Cache-Control: no-store`); older keys are not
  revoked. Format: `chronicle://host[:port]/path/c/<campaignID>?key=...` for an
  https base URL, `chronicle+http://` for http, built only by
  `BuildFoundryConnectLine`.
- **Presence.** `GET /campaigns/:id/foundry-presence` lives in the `foundry_vtt`
  plugin. Campaigns still owns the version-pin settings (`FoundryModulePin`,
  `FoundryModulePinMode` in `model.go`; `SetFoundryModulePin` /
  `GetFoundryModulePin` / `SetFoundryModulePinMode` / `GetFoundryModulePinMode`
  on `CampaignService`), which `foundry_vtt` reads through
  `foundryCampaignSettingsAdapter`. Extracting this coupling is #719.

## Business Rules

- Deleting a campaign (owner or site admin) moves it to the site Trash (`deleted_at`, `trash_repository.go`); it never hard-deletes. Every repository read filters on `deleted_at IS NULL`, and `FindMember` and `ListMembers` join the campaign (so the sessions token routes stop too), invite and transfer acceptance refuse it, and the Foundry auto-pin lists skip it, so campaign routes, the sync API, sockets and media all see "not found". Only the admin Trash page (admin plugin) can Undo (`RestoreFromTrash`) or purge (`PurgeTrashed`, which claims with `purge_started_at`, then runs the old delete flow: media files, hook, row). Owners have no Trash page.

- The invitation (campaign picture and name, members' names and pictures, next game night) shows to a signed-out person only for a link, emailed invite or code that is valid right now; anything else gets the plain page. It carries no emails, roles or GM-only content.
- Guest codes let someone with no account join one campaign as a player
  with only a name. Auth owns the guest account and the join page; this
  plugin owns the codes and the membership. A guest is shown as "Guest" with
  no email. Removing a guest, or purging their campaign from the Trash, ends their
  account (`OnMemberRemoved`, `OnCampaignDeleted`). Merging a guest into an account
  moves their membership (`MoveMembership`: an existing member keeps their
  role and gains the guest's character only if they had none).
- A guest code can instead be a look-around pass (1, 3 or 7 days,
  `visit_days`). Using it starts a visit (`visit_until`) and makes no
  membership: `AllowPublicCampaignAccess` admits the holder as `RoleNone` with
  `CampaignContext.VisitUntil` set, so they read what a public visitor would,
  on a private campaign too, and `RequireCampaignAccess` (every write) refuses
  them. The owner's card offers Make player (adds the membership, clears the
  pass) and End now (moves `visit_until` to now). Joining with a player's
  code clears the joiner's passes, and `RemoveMember` ends any still running,
  so a pass never outlives a membership. A merge carries the pass to the
  account. An ended pass leaves its guest account in place
  (`TODO(#2166)`).

- A new campaign starts with the site look: when an admin has chosen one
  (Admin > Site look), `Create` seeds the whole Appearance from that look
  (`look_presets.go`, read through the `SiteLookSource` interface), so the
  campaign renders as the look straight away. `lookPresets` mirrors the LOOKS
  table in `static/js/widgets/customize_look.js`; `test/js/fixtures/look_pins.json`
  pins both. Seeding runs the same validation as a Customize save. Classic is
  the default and stores nothing; a failed read never blocks creating a campaign.
- Campaign names must be non-empty (max 200 chars)
- Slug auto-generated from name, deduplicated with -2/-3 suffix
- Owner cannot remove self or change own role (must transfer first)
- Nobody becomes a member without their own yes: the People page's "Add a
  member" form makes an invite (`InviteService.CreateInvite`), and the
  campaign can be handed only to someone already in it (an email with no
  account gets the same answer as one outside the campaign).
- Removing a member replaces the join link when one is on, and records them in
  `settings.removed_member_ids`. `JoinByCode` turns those people away until the
  owner allows them back on the People page; an invite the owner sends them
  still works. Leaving by choice or deleting the account is not a removal.
- Ownership transfer: DB transaction swaps roles atomically
- Old owner becomes Scribe after transfer
- Admin force-transfer: admin joining as Owner demotes current owner to Scribe
- Regular member addition cannot assign Owner role (use transfer instead)
- Deleting a campaign cascades to all members, transfers, etc. (FK CASCADE)
- Campaign settings JSON stores which modules/plugins are enabled
- Settings writers save through `saveSettingsChanges`, which writes only the top-level keys they changed (`UpdateSettingsKeys`, one JSON_SET/JSON_REMOVE statement), so writers holding an older copy of the campaign can't undo each other (`settings_keys_integration_test.go`). `Update` (name, description, public) never writes settings.
- Default visibility for new content is `everyone` or `dm_only` (`is_private`
  bool). There is no creator-only "Private" tier — that option was removed
  per ADR-056; a campaign that still has `default_visibility:"private"`
  stored just collapses to `is_private=true` (DM Only) on read. A new
  campaign starts on `dm_only` (`newCampaignSettings`); existing campaigns
  keep what they have.

## Sidebar navigation

`nav.go` owns what a sidebar holds. `sidebar_config.items` is the one items
model: apps (`type:"app"`; `"addon"` is the older spelling, still read), categories,
the owner's sections (`type:"section"`) and links, each with `section` naming
where it sits (`pinned`, `apps`, `categories` or a section id). An app the
owner keeps in the app drawer only has `section:"drawer"`: it lands in
`NavLayout.Drawer`, no sidebar section draws it, and it is not re-added to Apps.

- `NormalizeNav` reads stored items into sections. Items without a `section`:
  an app or link under a section heading stays in it, any other app or link goes
  to Pinned, a category to Categories. Missing enabled apps and categories are
  added (a never-arranged campaign starts with Journal and Calendar pinned).
- `ViewNav` cuts that down for one viewer. `visible:false` means **hidden from
  players**: the owner still sees the row, marked; nobody else does, including an
  owner viewing as a player. Apps also need their addon on and the viewer's
  access (`NavAccess` mirrors each app route's gate). An app marked `Empty` (set
  by the composition root for Calendar when the viewer can see no calendar)
  reaches only the owner.
- A section heading with `visible:false` is no section for anyone:
  `NormalizeNav` omits it, its rows stay under the visible heading before it, and
  `validateSidebarItems` drops it on the next save. Its label can never reach a
  player.
- The sidebar HTML and the command palette's "Go to" list both come from
  `ViewNav`, so a hidden row cannot reach a player through either. Hiding a row
  does not make its pages private.
- `validateSidebarItems` guards `PUT /sidebar-config`: known types, ids, existing
  sections, label length, safe link URLs, Font Awesome link icons.
- `OrderNavCategories` is how AI Import's `menu-order` change reorders
  categories: the listed ones swap places among themselves within each section,
  so none changes section and other rows stay put. A category with no row gets
  one where the sidebar already draws it; a never-arranged sidebar first gets its
  default-pinned apps written, because any stored row makes the layout count as
  arranged.
- The app catalog (which addon turns each app on, its page and access, and its
  drawer group and line) is `navAppCatalog` in `internal/app/routes.go`, since
  it names other plugins.
- The app drawer lists every app the viewer may open: the app rows of their
  `ViewNav` sidebar plus `NavDrawerRows` (drawer-only apps they may open, minus
  their own pins), so it never offers what the sidebar withholds. The owner also
  gets the turned-off apps and the addon ids that switch each one
  (`buildNavDrawer` in `internal/app/nav_layout.go`). A member may pin a
  drawer-only app for themselves: `ViewNav` pulls a pinned key from the drawer
  into Pinned, and the pin check counts drawer rows. Drawn by
  `internal/templates/layouts/nav_drawer.templ`; `static/js/app_drawer.js` opens
  it, filters it and drags tiles onto Apps, and a tile's menu is the sidebar's
  row menu (`sidebar_row_menu.js`), whose drawer actions it runs.
- Drawing is the layout's: `internal/templates/layouts/nav.templ` draws given
  rows; `nav_state.go` works out where the viewer is. The current row is the one
  an entity page names via `layouts.WithNavHint` (its category), else the longest
  row URL the path starts with. The highlight is the look's nav style
  (`Appearance.EffectiveNavStyle`); a blank style means Classic's still rail on
  Classic and the living ring on other looks, so a style is stored only when it
  differs. Folds are remembered per viewer in the `chronicle_nav_folds` cookie
  (scoped to the campaign path) so the server paints them. `static/js/sidebar_nav.js`
  runs folding, moves the ring on boosted navigation (reads `[data-nav-current]`
  in `#main-content`) and keeps the phone drawer's focus.
- Customize's Icons section (Colours and type, between Buttons and Motion) stores
  `Appearance.IconAmount` (`few`, `names`, `all`) and `IconColour` (`ink`,
  `accent`, `category`); the defaults are stored empty. They are not part of a
  look (`LOOK_KEYS` in `customize_look.js`, `look_presets.go`), so starting from a
  look keeps them. The layout puts `data-cz-icons` and `data-cz-icon-colour` on
  `<html>` only off the defaults (`layouts.AppearanceAttrs`). A category's saved
  colour rides on `NavCategory.Color` and its views so the Category theme can read
  it; `layouts.KindIconAttrs` writes it as `--kind-colour`, only under that theme
  and only from a plain `#RRGGBB`.
- Hiding the sidebar slides it left; while hidden, resting the pointer on the
  window's left edge peeks it 70 px out (`static/js/sidebar_peek.js`; hover-capable
  screens 768 px and up). Empty space in the peek opens it fully.
- A category row is a button that slides the category drill panel over the
  sidebar (`GET /sidebar/drill/:slug`, `static/js/sidebar_drill.js`): entity
  tree, folders, favorites, recently viewed, tag filters. The category's own page
  stays reachable from the panel header. The sidebar itself has no page tree.
- Campaign settings is the owner's alone and is one sidebar row
  (`layouts.NavManageRows`, key `manage`) that leads to the desk. It is fixed at
  the foot of the sidebar above Site admin, outside every scroller
  (`campaignNavManage`). It is current on every owner tool page and says what
  needs the owner in words ("1 needs you", `GET /dashboard/needs`, asked for
  after the page draws, kept a minute, `sidebar_nav.js`); folded to icons it
  shows a dot. The palette's owner phrases (`ownerCommands`) carry `words` that
  also find them (the four page pieces point at their rows on Game & features;
  `manageToolWords` gives Trash "delete" and What players changed "history"). The tools (People, Media,
  Customize, Game & features, Foundry while sync-api is on, Your AI, Trash,
  Settings, What players changed) are reached from the desk's lines, from the
  command palette and from Find (`layouts.ManageToolRows` is the one list).
  Each page sits in `layouts.ManageFrame` (`internal/templates/layouts/manage_frame.templ`): one
  left edge and one content width, a quiet "Campaign settings" above the title that leads
  back to the desk, the title, the owner's pin and Find beside it, a slot for
  the page's own actions, then the page. The page's desk line (`Line`) draws
  its figures in muted ink beside the title ("5 members · 1 invited"; a state
  dot for Foundry and Your AI), built by `DeskService.Figure` from the same
  facts as the desk's row; the figure keeps `id="manage-figure"` so a removal
  or an invite swaps it in place, and a figure that cannot be read draws
  nothing. `NoFigure` opts a page out (Your AI changes its own state in place,
  where a figure would go stale).
  The Customizer is a full-screen workshop and is not framed.
- **Editing** is the owner's pencil (`static/js/sidebar_editor.js`) on one staged
  draft of the whole arrangement, carried only on the owner's page
  (`#sidebar-nav[data-nav-edit]`, `buildNavEdit` in `internal/app/nav_layout.go`;
  hidden rows and turned-off apps included). Save writes one `PUT /sidebar-config`
  (each owner section ahead of its rows, every row naming its section, turned-off
  apps kept in place), then swaps in the server's sidebar. Nothing is stored
  before Save; Cancel drops the draft. Pinned by `nav_editor_test.go` and
  `test/js/sidebar_editor.test.mjs`.
- **Member pins.** Players and scribes pin rows for themselves; `ViewNav` moves
  them into Pinned after the campaign's, marked as theirs. Stored in
  `campaign_members.nav_pins`, read for the viewer only (LayoutInjector).
  `PUT /nav-pins` checks every key
  against the caller's own sidebar (`NavSectionsSource`, `PinnableNavKeys`,
  `CleanNavPins`), so a pin cannot name a row hidden from players or an app they
  cannot open. The owner pins for everyone in the editor; for themselves they
  may pin owner tools only (`manage:*` keys, `CleanOwnerNavPins`), in the same
  column. `AppendManagePins` adds them to the owner's Pinned after `ViewNav`
  (they are not in any layout), skipping a tool whose add-on is off. The pin is
  a button beside an owner tool page's title
  (`layouts.ManagePin`, `static/js/manage_pin.js`), which hands the press to the
  sidebar's own `setPersonalPin`.
- **Row menu** (`static/js/sidebar_row_menu.js`; right-click, long press, dots
  button, Shift+F10 / Menu key). Owner: pin for everyone, hide from players,
  move up/down in the section, Edit sidebar; each saves at once through the
  editor's `quickChange` (same `PUT /sidebar-config`). Player/scribe: own pin.
  Link rows also offer a new tab. Shut while the owner edits.
  `test/js/sidebar_row_menu.test.mjs`.

written. Only "No game system" and "Keep this one" are passed on by hand
(`POST /campaigns/:id/setup-steps/:step/keep`, `KeepSetupStep`, stored as
`CampaignSettings.SetupKept`). The fragment answers empty once all four are done.
A co-DM (a Scribe with DM access) sees their own short note in its place
(`codmNoteHTML`), because the steps link to owner-only pages, and anyone else
sees nothing. Campaigns made before hold the older Text Block of steps in their
saved home; it is left as it is (`setupNoteFor` still swaps it for a co-DM).
`setup_steps` is a valid block type and has a registry entry but is not in the
Home pages palette (`homeBlockTypes`). Campaigns made before keep their
homes, and the guest home stays the built-in one.

A campaign made now (`Create`) also has the usual eleven features switched on
through the addons service (`FeatureEnabler`, whose list lives in `internal/app/starter_features.go`: Calendar,
Game nights, Journal, Jot notes, Maps, Timeline, Media Gallery, NPC Gallery,
Player Character Claiming, Attributes, Player Notes); Armory, Crafting,
Transformations and the Sync API start off. Import sets
`CreateCampaignInput.SkipDefaultFeatures` and applies the file's own addon list. New campaigns also save
a Players home (`playersHome`): the welcome banner over a 7 and 5 column row,
with Start here, Last time and Recently updated beside Coming up, the party,
When we play, Where we play and the house rules. Blocks stacked in one column
keep the rows' gap.

**Looking as someone else.** Home carries, under the title and for the real
owner and co-Directors only, the line `Dashboard for  You  Players  Guests`
(tab weight, `homePreviewChrome`). Each word posts to `POST /campaigns/:id/view-as`
(`as` = self, player, guest or guestcode; `next` = home or arrange; `home` = player or
guest, only with arrange; anything else is a 400; the gate is `CanAuthorDmOnly`,
and it works on an archived campaign). Home then becomes that dashboard where it
stands, under an amber note saying whose it is, with a quiet "Arrange this page"
for the owner (`as=self&next=arrange&home=...`, which leaves the preview first
because the Customizer is a members' page). One preview at a time; entering one
clears the other. `toggle-view-mode` (the header eye) turns any preview off,
otherwise starts the player preview. The eye shows on during every preview. The
shell strip reads "Viewing as player", or for a guest preview
`GuestPreviewBanner`: "Viewing as a visitor" or "Viewing as a visitor with a
guest code", a No code / Guest code switch (public campaigns only) and Back to
your view.

- **Player preview** is a display cookie (`chronicle_view_as_player`) whose
  value is the id of the campaign it was turned on for; both preview cookies
  are honoured only in that campaign. `ViewingAsPlayer` reads it; `CampaignShowPage` draws `cc.PlayerPreview()` and
  swaps to the Players home. Each block reads its viewer as a player.
- **Guest preview** is a downgrade made once in `AllowPublicCampaignAccess`
  (ADR-074), cookie `chronicle_view_as_guest` (session, HttpOnly, SameSite Lax).
  For a real owner or co-DM, on a GET/HEAD of a PUBLIC campaign, the request's
  `CampaignContext` is `GuestPreviewContext()`: a visitor's (`RoleNone`, not a
  member, no DM grant, not site admin, no visit pass), so every handler below runs
  the visitor's code path. `Preview`, `PreviewerRole` and `PreviewerCanDM` only
  draw the preview's chrome (`DMTeamChrome`, `OwnerChrome`) and never grant.
  Writes are never downgraded. The layout injector builds the shell from the
  visitor's values and notes `layouts.PreviewAs` for the eye and strip.
  `RequireCampaignAccess` (both gates) refuses a GET/HEAD of a members-only
  route during the preview as it would a non-member: a page gets a small page
  with a way back (`GuestPreviewRefusalPage`), a fragment or JSON read gets the
  403. The REST API's session door asks the same rule through
  `GuestPreviewAsked` and `GuestPreviewLowers`, so `/api/v1` answers a lowered
  read as it answers a visitor (401).
- **Guest-code preview** (ADR-076), cookie `chronicle_view_as_guestcode`, is the
  same downgrade with a look-around pass: `GuestCodePreviewContext()` adds a
  `VisitUntil` a week out, so the page is what someone who came in on a guest
  code sees (the visitor banner, the hero try-out). A pass opens a private
  campaign, so this preview works there too. `guestPreviewMode` decides which
  preview, if any, lowers a request; the code cookie wins over a stale guest one.
- **Private campaign:** the no-code preview never downgrades it, and asking for its guests' dashboard
  sets no cookie (`ViewAs` clears any preview and lands on
  `?dashboard=guests`, read by `GuestsNoteAsked`). Home then shows the line with
  Guests chosen and a note that only guests with a guest code can open it, with
  "See it as a guest with a code" (the guest-code preview), "Who can read it"
  and, for the owner, "Arrange this page". A cookie could only take effect
  later and unasked, on another campaign or once this one is made public.
- The preview keeps the owner signed in, so a grant made to the owner's own
  account on a page still matches where a signed-out visitor's would not.
- Owner-only controls in the home blocks ask `OwnerCardVisible(ctx, cc)`, which is
  false under either preview.

The Customizer names that tab "<GM term> team".

**Existing campaigns.** A Players home with no layout of its own still borrows
the Everyone home. The Customizer's editor state carries `suggest` for it
(`homeSuggestions`, the block types `playersHome` holds), and the editor draws
those the layout lacks as a faded draft above the page: Keep one block, Keep all
suggestions, or Dismiss draft. Nothing is in the layout or saved until a block is
kept, and a block whose add-on is off is not offered.

**Visibility.** A block shows only when its viewer can see something in it; the
owner sees every block. `activity_feed` and `sync_status` read owner-only routes,
so only the owner gets them: they ask `OwnerCardVisible` themselves (owner, and
not while looking as a player), mark their card `data-dm-only`, and so draw
nothing for anyone else on whatever page they sit; the sync fragment route is
owner-only too. `category_grid` omits categories with no page the
viewer can open (`dashVisibleCategories`); under Annals it draws them as one
wrapping line of words, name then count, and past `categoryLineMax` the rest
wait behind "and N more". Calendar cards show "isn't enabled"
to the owner alone, link into the members-only calendar only for members, and
mark their lazy slot `data-drop-if-empty`, which `boot.js` answers by removing the
block when the fragment comes back empty.

Blocks carry the shared parts (`cb-title`, `cb-head`, `cb-link`, `cb-card`, plus
`cb-hero`, `cb-epi`, `cb-quiet`, `cb-shortcut`, `cb-row`), so the Annals page look
turns the home into a front page (`page_look_annals.css`, "The campaign home").

### The desk's provider seam

Other plugins fill rows through `DeskProvider` (`Slot()` and `Facts()`), registered with `Handler.RegisterDeskProvider` from `internal/app/routes.go`, the same inversion as `RegisterSettingsTab`; campaigns never imports them. Slots: changes (playerchanges), media, trash (entities), AI (ai_workspace), keys (syncapi, the count of the campaign's active API keys), foundry (this package, over `FoundryConnector`). Each provider runs under a 2 second deadline on its own goroutine, a panic or error is logged and the row shows "Open" (a tool page draws no figure), and a result is merged only under the provider's own slot. Providers read only the campaign the owner-only request names. Blanks (`desk_blank.js`, loaded by the blank's own inline handler on first use) save through `PUT /default-visibility`, `PUT /details` (Name, Description and Campaign) and `PUT /system` (System, body `{"system_id": id}`), and a member's role through `PUT /members/:uid/role`, which answers a redirect (`data-expect="redirect"`).


## Export / import fidelity

Read before touching `export_service.go`, `internal/app/export_adapters.go`,
`import_report.go` or `import.templ`.

- **Import is best-effort and reports losses.** One unparseable row must not
  abandon a half-built campaign, and a partial import must not claim full
  success. `ImportReport` is threaded through the importer adapters like `*IDMap`
  and is a **return value, not a side channel**: `Import` returns
  `(*Campaign, *ImportReport, error)`. A lossy import renders `ImportResultPanel`
  ("Imported with losses", tally, per-object list) instead of the success
  redirect.
  - Record degraded-but-created rows too, not just hard failures.
  - Detail is capped at 200 records (`maxRecordedImportFailures`); the count stays
    exact past the cap. `FailN` records a homogeneous batch as one row with a
    real count.
  - Section/kind labels come from constants in `import_report.go`, not inline
    literals (plugin-slug strings outside the owning plugin directory fail
    `tools/check-plugin-isolation.sh`).
- **Shared notes travel; egress is narrow.** `SetNoteExporter` / `SetNoteImporter`
  wire `internal/widgets/notes`' `ListSharedByCampaign`. Only `is_shared=TRUE`
  notes leave; `shared_with` targets are dropped; personal notes never leave (see
  `internal/widgets/notes/.ai.md`). `internal/wire/export_adapter_wiring_test.go`
  is an AST pin requiring `routes.go` to call all twenty-seven export/import setters.
- **Rolling tables, quest sheets and crafting recipes travel as their plugins'
  own JSON** (`roll_tables`, `quests`, `recipes`), wired in `routes.go` beside
  each plugin because those services are built after the rest. Import goes
  through the plugin's validation, never straight to a table: tables are stored
  as one document and refused whole; a recipe is created, then its rules, looks
  and extras are saved on top, and the service checks its item and character
  pages exist; a sheet is stored by `quests.Archive`, which drops (and reports)
  any page, map or due-date link that did not come back and creates no calendar
  events. Page ids are remapped through `IDMap.EntityIDs`, map ids through
  `IDMap.MapIDs`, a due-date event through `IDMap.CalendarEventIDs`, so quests
  import after pages, maps and the calendar. Not yet carried: quest boards,
  crafting benches, settings, spoils and projects.
- **ZIP import restores pictures.** `?include_media=1` writes `campaign.json` plus
  `media/<basename>` (stored names carry date folders; the entry is the
  `<uuid>.<ext>` basename, named by `ExportMediaFile.Filename`). On import, media
  is restored first through `MediaService.Upload` (same type, size and quota
  checks as any upload); `remapMediaReferences` then replaces each restored
  file's old id with its new one across the envelope (portraits, covers, map
  backgrounds, tokens, `/media/<id>` in any text). An id with no restored file
  is left alone. A manifest without `Filename` is paired by id. A JSON upload
  carries no bytes; the report counts missing files and pages whose picture did
  not come back.
- **Add-ons import first; the file's categories replace the defaults.** An add-on
  gates what may be created (the Player Character category is refused while its
  addon is off), so `Import` restores add-ons before entity types. A file that
  carries categories creates the campaign with `SkipEntityTypeSeed`, so defaults
  do not arrive twice. A category an add-on already made (premade Player
  Character type, a game system's presets) is reused, matched by the PC rule or
  slug. Types export `preset_category`, `parent_type_slug` and `claimable`, which
  keeps a PC category claimable and nested on restore.
- **Page ids are remapped wherever the campaign names them.** `sidebar_config` and
  each category's description and `pinned_entity_ids` are written after every
  page exists: `remapSidebarIDs` (`import_sidebar.go`) and the importer adapter
  map category and page ids to new ones and drop any whose original did not come
  back (the report lists it). Timeline events export from the full event, so
  description, times of day and repeat travel. A calendar's `tracks_real_time` and
  `real_time_zone` travel; import enables real time only for a zone this server
  can load and reports any other.
- Exports use the entities repository's paginated readers (see `entities/.ai.md`
  for the total ordering they need).

## Leaving a campaign

- Each card on My campaigns has three dots: Open and "Leave campaign…" (a
  player, scribe or co-DM), or Open and "Hand over…" (the owner, whose card
  links to `/campaigns/:id/transfer`). Ownership comes from the membership
  role (`OwnedMemberships`), not from who created the campaign.
- Leaving writes nothing but the membership and the person's group places.
  Posts, shared notes, private notes, the journal and rolls stay where they
  are; a rejoin finds them again. Characters become free to pick
  (`owner_user_id` cleared, `playable` kept on); the owner decides who gets
  them next. The owner, and only the owner, gets one bell note.
- A guest cannot leave: their account exists for the campaign and would end.
