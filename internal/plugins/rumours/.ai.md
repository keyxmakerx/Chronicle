# Rumours Plugin

## Purpose

The Rumours page block (`rumours`, one per page): what the party has heard
about a page, who said it, how sure they were, what the Director knows is true
and what the party believes. Truth and belief are one joined mark at the foot of
each rumour. Registered for the `template` context in `internal/app/routes.go`
behind the plugin's health gate; works on any page type.

## Who may do what

| | Read | Add | Edit words | Remove | Mark what the party thinks | Say what is true |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Player | yes (no truth) | no | no | no | no | no |
| Scribe | yes (no truth) | yes | own only | own only | yes | **no** |
| Director / granted co-Director | yes, with truth | yes | any | any | yes | yes |

The role is `CampaignContext.VisibilityRole()`, so a granted co-Director is the
Director. A Scribe can never change the Director's truth: the truth route is
`directorOnly()`, the service refuses it again, and no other write touches the
`truth` column.

## Where the truth is kept from everyone else

Three layers, any one of which is enough:

1. SQL: `selectRumour(withTruth=false)` selects `''` in place of `r.truth`.
2. Service: `viewOf` attaches `Truth` only for the Director.
3. Markup: the Director's pair, its DM tag and its menus are rendered only when
   `View.IsDM`; nothing for anyone else, so there is no empty slot.

A player who has nothing to read and cannot add gets `View.Hidden` and the
block draws nothing, so the page has no gap.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `embed.go`, `migrations/001_page_rumours.*.sql` | slug `rumours`, its own embedded schema (idempotent) |
| `model.go` | constants and limits, `Viewer`, `Rumour`, `RumourView`, `View`, inputs (`UpdateRumourInput` is `patch.Field`-based) |
| `repository.go` | hand-written SQL, the cap under a lock on the page row |
| `service.go` | every rule about who sees and writes what; plain-language validation messages |
| `handler.go` | bind, open the page through `Pages`, call the service; route gates |
| `view.go`, `rumours_block.templ` | the block's markup; controls are inline IIFEs (`call`, `valueCall`), never `templ script` |
| `static/js/rumours.js`, `static/css/rumours.css` | the widget and its look; rows are drawn from a JSON script with `textContent` |

`Pages` (implemented in `internal/app/rumours_adapters.go` over the entities
service) is how the plugin asks whether the caller may open a page; a missing,
foreign or hidden page is 404.

## Routes (JSON)

All under `/campaigns/:id/entities/:eid/rumours`.

| Method | Path | Who |
|--------|------|-----|
| GET | `` | any member who can open the page |
| POST | `` | Scribe, Director |
| PUT | `/:rid` | Scribe (own), Director; partial: `quote`, `who`, `sure` |
| PUT | `/:rid/truth` | Director |
| PUT | `/:rid/belief` | Scribe, Director |
| DELETE | `/:rid` | Scribe (own), Director; 204 |

## Bounds

`MaxRumours` (200) is the cap on a page, the `LIMIT` of every read and the
reason for the block's cut notice: when the page holds more than was sent, the
block says "Showing the newest N of M rumours on this page."

## Known limits

- "When" is the date the rumour was added; sessions have no numeric label to
  show.
- Names are read live from `users.display_name`.
