# Decks Plugin

## Purpose

Card decks for tabletop campaigns, behind the **Card decks** add-on
(`decks`, off until a campaign turns it on). Server only for now: tables,
repository, service and JSON routes; the screens are separate. Registered in
`internal/app/routes.go` behind the plugin's health gate; the migration is the
plugin's own (`migrations/001_decks`), new tables only. See ADR-076.

## A deck

One kind of thing with settings. `kind` (`hero`, `party`, `event`) only sets
defaults; a hero deck may name a hero page (`holderEntityId`).

A **deck** has **cards**. A card is a definition (name, words, kind label,
copies, fate...). A **copy** is one physical card: a card at 3 copies has 3
rows in `deck_copies`. Copies, never weights.

### Settings

Normalized by the service (`settings.go`); unknown keys are dropped and the
stored text is a re-marshal of the typed struct.

| Key | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `draws` | `directors`, `players` | `directors` |
| `knows` (what a player who sees the deck may know) | `nothing`, `count`, `kinds`, `faces` | `count` |
| `othersSee` (what others see of a card dealt to one hero) | `nothing`, `back`, `face` | `back` |
| `arrives` (a card dealt to a hero) | `down`, `up` | `down` for hero decks, else `up` |
| `fate` (after a card is played) | `back`, `used`, `gone`, `play` | `used` |
| `empty` (a draw from an empty pile) | `reshuffle`, `stop` | `stop` |
| `discard` (players discard from their own hand) | bool | false |
| `notice` | `slide`, `count`, `quiet` | `slide` for hero decks, else `count` |

`notice` is stored and checked only; nothing reads it yet.

### Look

Every value comes from a fixed list (one list per key in `settings.go`, the
first value its default; a stored value outside the list reads as the default); no hex colour is stored (the stylesheet
resolves a palette key for light and dark).

- `face`: `plate`, `window`, `full` (default `plate`)
- `shape`: `tall`, `standard`, `small` (default `tall`)
- `border`: `double`, `line`, `kit`, `metal`, `none` (default `double`)
- `turn`: `flip`, `slow`, `toss`, `slam`, `sweep`, `burn`, `ink` (default `flip`)
- `back`: `{pattern, colour, picture, words}`; pattern `lattice`, `stripes`,
  `dots`, `stars`, `plain` (default `lattice`); colour a palette key or `""`
  (the campaign's accent); picture `""` for now; words at most 24 characters
- `kinds`: at most 12 of `{key, name, colour}`; key `^[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,31}$`,
  unique; name 1 to 24; colour a palette key
- palette (`Palette` in `settings.go`): `violet`, `indigo`, `blue`, `cyan`,
  `teal`, `moss`, `plum`, `rose`, `umber`, `slate`

A card's `kindKey` is `""` or a key in the deck's `kinds`; a kind a card still
uses cannot be dropped. A card's `picture` and `when` accept only `""` for now
(`when_open` is stored).

## Zones and moves

A copy is in exactly one zone: `pile`, `shown` (drawn and shown to the
Directors, not yet dealt), `hand` (of a hero page), `play` (on the table; of a
hero page, or of nobody, the whole table), `used`, `gone`.

The pile has no stored order. A draw picks uniformly at random among the
pile's copies at that moment (`crypto/rand`, `cryptoPick`), inside one
transaction that first takes the deck row's lock, so draws, shuffles, gathers
and copies changes on one deck run one after another.

Every move claims its copy with a conditional update on its zone, holder and
face (and the asked mark where it matters) and checks the rows changed; a lost
race is a 409 and writes and announces nothing. A move and its log line are
one transaction.

| Verb | From | To | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| draw (Director) | pile | `shown` (at most one per deck) | Director |
| draw (player, heroId in the body) | pile | the hero's hand | a chosen player of a `draws: players` deck with draws left; spends one |
| `deal` | shown | a hero's hand (`arrives`) or the table (`play`, face up) | Director |
| deal-card | a named card's pile copies | same targets | Director |
| `put-back` | shown | pile | Director |
| `another` | shown | pile, then a new draw to shown, never the same copy while another exists | Director |
| `turn` | hand | same hand, flipped | Director; the owner of that hero |
| `play` | hand | by fate | Director; the owner. An `askFirst` card played by a player sets `asked_at` and waits |
| `approve` | hand, asked | by fate | Director |
| `turn-down` | hand, asked | same hand, mark cleared | Director |
| `take-off` | play | by fate (`play` ends in `used`) | Director; the owner of the hero it is in front of (not a table card) |
| `discard` | hand | by fate (`play` ends in `used`) | Director; the owner when the deck's `discard` is on |
| `take-back` | hand, play | pile | Director; logged with `seen` when it was face up |
| shuffle | used | pile | Director |
| gather | every zone | pile | Director |

Fate is the card's own, or the deck's when the card's is `""`: `back` to the
pile, `used`, `gone`. Fate `play` moves a played card to `play` in front of
that hero. A card dealt to the table is in `play` with no holder, face up, and
ends in `used` when taken off.

A draw from an empty pile: with `empty: reshuffle` and a non-empty used pile
the used pile is shuffled back first; otherwise a 409, "Nothing left in this
deck." A draw that fails spends nothing.

Lowering a card's `copies` removes copies from the pile, then used, then
gone; if more would have to leave a hand, play or the shown spot it is refused
with a message naming how many are held. Raising adds pile copies. Deleting a
card or deck removes its copies wherever they are. At most 60 decks per
campaign, 200 cards per deck, 600 copies per deck.

The log (`deck_log`) keeps the newest 1000 lines per deck and is for Directors.

## Who may do what

"Director" is `CampaignContext.CanAuthorDmOnly()` (owner or granted
co-Director). "Their hero" is a page of this campaign the viewer is the claimed
owner of, read live through the `Heroes` interface (`directory.go`),
implemented in `internal/app/decks_adapters.go` over the entities service.
Every player rule needs a current member of at least Player role (a Scribe is a
player here); a former member or a visitor to a public campaign sees nothing.

A deck, card or copy the viewer may not see answers exactly like a missing one
(404), on every route. A thing they can see but may not act on is 403.
Update bodies are partial (`patch.Field`): an absent key keeps, an explicit
null clears the hero page, a value replaces; a deck update carries the
`version` it loaded and a stale one is a 409. Chosen players are saved with the
version too.

## What a player is sent

A deck is listed to a player if they are one of its chosen players, a hero
they own is its holder or holds a copy of it in hand or play, or it is a
`party` deck. Event decks are otherwise never listed. A grant made before the
member's `joined_at` counts for nothing (`request.chosen`).

`PlayerDeck` (`model.go`) is its own type, not a Director type with fields
blanked, so a hidden field cannot be forgotten: name, kind, look, `canDraw`,
`drawsLeft`, the player's own heroes' hands and cards in play, and by `knows`
nothing more / the pile count / plus the count per kind / plus every card's
face. By `othersSee`, for other heroes' held cards: nothing / a count per hero /
also the faces of their face-up cards; a hero page the viewer cannot open is
not named. A party deck adds its used pile.

A face-down copy is its id, its deck and `faceUp: false`, and nothing else. A
player is never sent a `director_note`, `when_json`, a condition, the pile's,
`gone`'s or a non-party used pile's contents, another deck's existence or the
log. Add a field to a player type only on purpose, and with a leak test
(`leak_test.go`).

`Table` returns, to any member, the face-up copies in `play` with no holder,
from every deck including event decks (so a table card names its deck).

## Routes

All under `/campaigns/:id`, signed in, Player role or above, add-on on. JSON.

| Method | Path | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| GET, POST | `/decks` | GET: the Director list, or the player list; POST: Director |
| GET | `/decks/table` | any member |
| GET, PUT, DELETE | `/decks/:did` | PUT and DELETE: Director |
| PUT | `/decks/:did/players` | Director: `{version, players: [{userId, drawsLeft}]}` |
| POST | `/decks/:did/cards` | Director |
| PUT, DELETE | `/decks/:did/cards/:cid` | Director |
| POST | `/decks/:did/draw` | Director draws to shown; a player names `heroId` |
| POST | `/decks/:did/copies/:copy/:verb` | verbs above; `deal` takes `{to: hand\|table, heroId}` |
| POST | `/decks/:did/deal-card` | Director: `{cardId, to, heroId}` |
| POST | `/decks/:did/shuffle`, `/decks/:did/gather` | Director |

Every write returns the deck as the same viewer now sees it (`ActionResult`:
the touched `copyId` and the `deck`). The `Notifier` (`WithNotifier`) is told
after every stored move; nothing is wired to it yet.

## Leak-hardening rules

- A copy that returns to the pile (take back, put back a shown card, reshuffle on an empty pile, shuffle, gather) gets a fresh id, so an id a player once saw cannot be followed back through the pile. `ActionResult.CopyID` is empty for a move to the pile. The log keeps `card_name` so its lines still read by card.
- A player's kind counts include every copy whose face the player cannot see: the pile, shown cards, used and gone cards of a deck that is not a party deck, and other heroes' cards unless visible to them.
- Copy lists in player replies are ordered by arrival (`moved_at`), then id, never by card.
- A player turns a card face up only; a repeat answers 409 and writes no log line or notice. A Director may turn it back. A player may play only a face-up card.
- The five move routes carry a per-user limit of 120 a minute (`middleware.UserRateLimit`).
- Saving a look refuses an unknown key with a message naming it; reading a stored look falls back field by field, validates kinds one at a time and re-checks back words.
- A `Notifier` notice to a player may name only the deck; anything naming a copy, card or holder is for Directors.
