# Decks Plugin

## Purpose

Card decks for tabletop campaigns, behind the **Card decks** add-on
(`decks`, off until a campaign turns it on). Tables, repository, service and
JSON routes, the card every screen draws (see "The card"), and the Director's
workshop pages (see "The Director's workshop"). Registered in
`internal/app/routes.go` behind the plugin's health gate; the migrations are the
plugin's own (`migrations/`), new tables only. See ADR-077.

## A deck

One kind of thing with settings. `kind` (`hero`, `party`, `event`) only sets
defaults; a hero deck may name a hero page (`holderEntityId`).

A **deck** has **cards**. A card is a definition (name, words, kind label,
corner text, `rare`, copies, fate...). A **copy** is one physical card: a card at 3 copies has 3
rows in `deck_copies`. Copies, never weights.

### Settings

Normalized by the service (`settings.go`); unknown keys are dropped and the
stored text is a re-marshal of the typed struct.

| Key | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `draws` | `directors`, `players` | `directors` |
| `knows` (what a player who sees the deck may know) | `nothing`, `count`, `kinds`, `faces` | `count` |
| `othersSee` (what others see of a card dealt to one hero) | `nothing`, `back`, `face` | `back` |
| `arrives` (a card dealt to a hero) | `down`, `up` | `down` for hero decks, else `up` |
| `fate` (after a card is played) | `back`, `used`, `gone`, `play` | `used` |
| `empty` (a draw from an empty pile) | `reshuffle`, `stop` | `stop` |
| `discard` (players discard from their own hand) | bool | false |
| `notice` | `slide`, `count`, `quiet` | `slide` for hero decks, else `count` |

`notice` is how loudly a card's moment tells the Director: `quiet` decks are not watched at all, and a moment within 5 minutes of the last one is marked quiet (see Cards that wait for their moment).

### Look

Every value comes from a fixed list (one list per key in `settings.go`, the
first value its default; a stored value outside the list reads as the default,
field by field); no hex colour is stored (the stylesheet resolves a palette
key for light and dark). Saving a look refuses an unknown key or value by name.

- `shape`: `tall`, `standard`, `small` (default `tall`). The shape decides the
  build (tall is the plate and the leaf, the others the compact card); there
  is no `face` key. A stored look that still has one reads without error and
  the key is dropped
- `border`: `double`, `line`, `kit`, `metal`, `brass`, `steel`, `bound`,
  `stone`, `none` (default `double`)
- `turn`: `flip`, `slow`, `toss`, `slam`, `sweep`, `burn`, `ink`, `seal`,
  `bolt`, `forge` (default `flip`)
- `window` (drawn only on tall cards): `arch`, `square`, `pointed`, `round`,
  `full`
- `name` (where the name sits): `below`, `over`
- `lettering`: `plain`, `carved`, `classic`, `condensed`, `hand`
- `corner` (the style of the corner mark, not a card's corner text): `roman`,
  `number`, `suit`, `pips`, `none`
- `marker` (how a card shows its kind): `gem`, `banner`, `tab`, `ribbon`, `line`
- `finish`: `matte`, `glow`, `sheen`, `foil`
- `back`: `{pattern, sign, colour, picture, words}`; pattern `lattice`, `rays`,
  `waves`, `stars`, `check`, `plain`, `iron` (default `lattice`); sign `star`,
  `sun`, `moon`, `eye`, `crown`, `anchor`, `flame`, `sword`, `none` (default
  `star`); colour a palette key or `""` (the campaign's accent); picture `""`
  for now; words at most 24 characters
- `kinds`: at most 12 of `{key, name, colour, sign}`; key
  `^[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,31}$`, unique; name 1 to 24; colour a palette key; sign
  one of `round`, `diamond`, `point`, `square`, `star`, `cross`, `crescent`,
  `drop`. A kind saved without a sign gets the first sign no other kind of the
  deck wears (with more kinds than signs, the least worn)
- palette (`Palette` in `settings.go`): `violet`, `indigo`, `blue`, `cyan`,
  `teal`, `moss`, `plum`, `rose`, `umber`, `slate`

On a read, a kind with a retired colour keeps its place with the first palette
colour; a kind with no usable key or name is dropped.

A card's `kindKey` is `""` or a key in the deck's `kinds`; a kind a card still
uses cannot be dropped. A card's `when` is a list of up to four tests (see Cards that wait for their moment); `when_open` is derived from it, never sent.
A card's `picture` is a media file id of this campaign (see Card pictures). A card's `rare` flag is part of its face: it is in
`Face` and the Director's card, and never sent for a face-down copy.

### The draw nudges

A Director may name one card to come up next (`nextCardId`) and one to come up
last (`lastCardId`), set through the deck update (absent keeps, null or `""`
clears; each must be a card of the deck). A random draw takes the next card
first, if a copy of it is in the pile, and then clears the field; the last
card's copies are left out of a draw while any other copy is in the pile, and
the field clears when one is drawn. With a count above one, next is the first
card of the draw, and the last card is drawn within it once nothing else is left. A named deal (`deal-card`) ignores both. A player's draw
obeys them exactly as a Director's does. Neither field is on any player type,
nothing a player reads changes because one is set, and deleting the named card
clears the field. A draw that clears one does not bump the deck's version, and
an update that names neither never writes them back. A pick is stored only if
the card still belongs to the deck when the update runs (under the deck lock);
otherwise it is stored as none, and the reply shows what was stored.

## Card pictures

A card's `picture` is a campaign media file id. It is checked on create and on
an update that changes it (the stored one was checked when it was set), through
`Pictures` (`directory.go`), implemented in `internal/app/decks_adapters.go`
over the media service: an image of this campaign, not a note picture or page
file, not in the campaign Trash. A URL, a path, a made-up id and any unusable
file all get one refusal wording. The back's picture stays `""`.

A `Face` carries the id in `picture` (the page loads `/media/<id>` with its own
session); a face-down copy carries nothing of it. The media server decides who
may fetch the file (ADR-078): `Service.IsCardPicture`, `CardPictureSeenBy`,
`CardPictureIDs` and `CardPictureUses` are what the media plugin's
`CardPictureGuard` asks, through an adapter in `internal/app`.
`CardPictureSeenBy` builds the player deck the viewer would be sent (and the
table) and looks for a card wearing the picture in it, so a picture is open
exactly when its face is; Directors always. `keep` copies the id into the
target deck. Where the media file is deleted for good the card keeps the id and
renders without a picture.

## Zones and moves

A copy is in exactly one zone: `pile`, `shown` (drawn and shown to the
Directors, not yet dealt; at most five copies of a deck at once), `hand` (of a
hero page, or on a party deck held by no hero: the party's hand), `play` (on the table; of a
hero page, or of nobody, the whole table), `used`, `gone`.

The pile has no stored order. A draw picks uniformly at random among the
pile's copies at that moment (`crypto/rand`, `cryptoPick`), inside one
transaction that first takes the deck row's lock, so draws, shuffles, gathers
and copies changes on one deck run one after another.

Every move claims its copy with a conditional update on its zone, holder and
face (and the asked mark where it matters) and checks the rows changed; a lost
race is a 409 and writes and announces nothing. A move and its log line are
one transaction.

| Verb | From | To | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| draw (Director, `count` 1 to 5) | pile | `shown` (at most five per deck; a draw that would pass five is refused whole; a count above what the pile holds draws what is there) | Director |
| draw (player, heroId in the body) | pile | the hero's hand | a chosen player of a `draws: players` deck with draws left; spends one |
| `deal` | shown | a hero's hand (`arrives`), the table (`play`, face up), or on a party deck with no hero the party's hand | Director |
| deal-card | a named card's pile copies | same targets | Director |
| `put-back` | shown | pile | Director |
| `another` | shown | that copy to the pile, then one new draw to shown, never the same card while another exists | Director |
| `turn` | hand | same hand, flipped; private (see below) | Director; the owner of that hero; any member, in the party's hand |
| `show` | hand, face up | same hand, `open` | Director; the owner of that hero; a player cannot undo it |
| `play` | hand | by fate | Director; the owner (any member, in the party's hand). An `askFirst` card played by a player sets `asked_at` and waits, put to the Director as one ask (see "The Director's side of asks") |
| `approve` | hand, asked | by fate | Director; answers the ask that waits for the copy, when one does |
| `turn-down` | hand, asked | same hand, mark cleared | Director; the same |
| `take-off` | play | by fate (`play` ends in `used`) | Director; the owner of the hero it is in front of (not a table card) |
| `discard` | hand | by fate (`play` ends in `used`) | Director; the owner (any member, in the party's hand) when the deck's `discard` is on and the card is face up |
| `take-back` | hand, play | pile | Director; logged with `seen` when it was face up |
| `keep` (`targetDeckId`) | shown, hand, play | `gone`; a copy is added to another deck's pile | Director |
| shuffle | used | pile | Director |
| gather | every zone | pile | Director |

**Turning and showing are two marks.** `face_up` is what the holder (and
Directors) see; `is_open` is the holder's second step of showing a face-up card
in a hand to the table. A player turning a card changes nothing anyone else is
sent, so a turned and an unturned card in someone else's hand are the same
bytes. On a deck whose `othersSee` is `face`, other players read the face of a
copy in a hand only when it is open (a card played in front of a hero is on
the table, so face up is enough). `is_open` implies `face_up`, is cleared when
the card is turned down or leaves the hand, and is shown to Directors beside
`faceUp`. Arriving face up still means face up for the holder only.

**The party's hand** (a party deck, zone `hand`, no holder). A hand deal with
no hero lands there; on a hero or event deck it stays a 422. Every member who
may see the deck sees it, face-down copies as the same face-down entry as
anywhere (`partyHand` on `PlayerDeck`), and a face-up copy is read by every
member because the whole party holds it. Any member may turn, play (ask-first
applies) and, when the deck allows discards, discard from it. `show` there is a
409: there is nobody left to show it to.

**Keep for good.** `keep` copies the card into another deck of the campaign as
a new card with one pile copy, or adds one copy to the card of that name the
target already has. Name, words, picture, corner, rare, fate, ask-first and
the Director's note carry over; the kind only if the target has that key; the
condition (`when_json`, `when_open`, `said_at`) does not, because its tests name pages and tags of the source deck. The source copy goes to `gone`.
The target's limits (200 cards, 600 copies, 20 per card) apply, and a refusal
changes nothing: one transaction holding both decks' locks in id order. Every
writer of a deck's cards (create, edit, keep) holds that deck's lock, so the
limits are counted against committed state; a keep whose insert still meets a
duplicate name is a retryable 409.

Fate is the card's own, or the deck's when the card's is `""`: `back` to the
pile, `used`, `gone`. Fate `play` moves a played card to `play` in front of
that hero. A card dealt to the table is in `play` with no holder, face up, and
ends in `used` when taken off.

A draw from an empty pile: with `empty: reshuffle` and a non-empty used pile
the used pile is shuffled back first; otherwise a 409, "Nothing left in this
deck." A Director's draw of several takes what the pile holds (at least one),
so its reply can list fewer copies than the count; only a pile with nothing
drawable is the 409. A draw that fails, or would pass five shown, takes and
spends nothing. The draw reply lists the copies it took (`copyIds`, a list
even for one card, and for a player's draw too).

An edit writes `copies` and reconciles the copy rows only when its body
carries `copies`, under the deck lock, so a keep that raised the count
meanwhile is never undone by an edit of something else. Lowering a card's `copies` removes copies from the pile, then used, then
gone; if more would have to leave a hand, play or the shown spot it is refused
with a message naming how many are held. Raising adds pile copies. Deleting a
card or deck removes its copies wherever they are. At most 60 decks per
campaign, 200 cards per deck, 600 copies per deck.

The log (`deck_log`) keeps the newest 1000 lines per deck and is for Directors.
Its action names are stable and plain because later they become rule
triggers: `drawn`, `dealt`, `turned`, `shown`, `played`, `asked`, `approved`,
`turned-down`, `taken-off`, `discarded`, `taken-back`, `put-back`, `kept`,
`shuffled`, `gathered`. `another` writes a `put-back` line and a `drawn` line.

## Who may do what

"Director" is `CampaignContext.CanAuthorDmOnly()` (owner or granted
co-Director). "Their hero" is a page of this campaign the viewer is the claimed
owner of, read live through the `Heroes` interface (`directory.go`),
implemented in `internal/app/decks_adapters.go` over the entities service.
Every player rule needs a current member of at least Player role (a Scribe is a
player here); a former member or a visitor to a public campaign sees nothing.

A deck, card or copy the viewer may not see answers exactly like a missing one
(404), on every route. A thing they can see but may not act on is 403.
Update bodies are partial (`patch.Field`): an absent key keeps, an explicit
null clears the hero page, a value replaces; a deck update carries the
`version` it loaded and a stale one is a 409. Chosen players are saved with the
version too.

## What a player is sent

A deck is listed to a player if they are one of its chosen players, a hero
they own is its holder or holds a copy of it in hand or play, or it is a
`party` deck. Event decks are otherwise never listed. A grant made before the
member's `joined_at` counts for nothing (`request.chosen`).

`PlayerDeck` (`model.go`) is its own type, not a Director type with fields
blanked, so a hidden field cannot be forgotten: name, kind, look, `canDraw`,
`drawsLeft`, the player's own heroes' hands and cards in play, and by `knows`
nothing more / the pile count / plus the count per kind / plus every card's
face. By `othersSee`, for other heroes' held cards: nothing / a count per hero /
also the faces of their open cards (see above); a hero page the viewer cannot
open is not named. A party deck adds its used pile and its hand.

A face-down copy is its id, its deck, `faceUp: false` and `waiting: false`,
and nothing else: no `rare`, no `open`. A
player is never sent a `director_note`, `when_json`, a condition, the pile's,
`gone`'s or a non-party used pile's contents, another deck's existence or the
log. Add a field to a player type only on purpose, and with a leak test
(`leak_test.go`).

`Table` returns, to any member, the face-up copies in `play` with no holder,
from every deck including event decks (so a table card names its deck).

## The card

One shared object, drawn by `CardView(look, state, opts)` and moved by one
script. A face-down card is `FaceDown()`, which takes no card data, and
`CardBack(look, size)` takes only the deck's look and a size, so a back cannot
carry a name, words, kind, picture or id; a face-up card is
`FaceUp(face, picture)`. The shape decides the build (the tall plate with its
`CardLeaf` at the open size, or the compact card); the size (`row`, `hand`,
`open`) changes how many lines show, never the type size, and the hand size
(about 92px) thins every frame and hides the corner mark. A name is clamped on
`.dk-nt`, the span inside `.dk-name`, whose height is a whole number of lines
(`--dk-nl` lines at `1lh` each); the padded `.dk-name` never clamps, because a
padded box shows the next line in its padding.

- A stored key becomes a class only through a lookup table in `card_look.go`,
  one per list in `settings.go`; `card_look_test.go` fails a key with no class
  or no stylesheet rule, so a key added on the server without a look cannot ship.
- `static/css/decks_card.css` (one prefix, `dk-`) holds the look. Colours are
  Chronicle's tokens except the fixed `MATERIALS` (iron, brass, steel, stone,
  gold) and the kind `PALETTE`, which read the same in light and dark.
- `static/js/deck_cards.js` (widget `deck-cards`, mounted by each card's
  `data-widget`) owns every move: `turnOver` plays the deck's turn-over, and
  `move`/`deal`/`shuffle` play the fixed moves. It follows `html[data-motion]`,
  asks for a face only when a turn starts, waits at the halfway point for at
  most 400ms, and ends every move on the plain resting card.
- The kind is named in words and drawn as a sign, never by colour alone.
## The Director's workshop

Server-rendered Templ pages for Directors only; a player or scribe gets 404
(`h.director` runs before anything is read). With the add-on off, a full page
is redirected to the campaign by `RequireAddon`, and an HTMX or JSON request
gets 404. The app's drawer row (`Card decks`, `NavAccessDirector` in
`campaigns/nav.go`) is shown only to a member who can write Director-only
content.

- **The app page** (`/decks/workshop`, `workshop_list.templ`): the decks, and
  Make a deck.
- **Making a deck** (`/decks/workshop/new`, `workshop_new.templ`): a name, who
  it is for (a hero, the party, events; the hero list is `Roster.Heroes`) and
  three ways to start, as tabs (the deck page's `.dkw-tab` strip; its arrow,
  Home and End keys are `tabKeyTarget` in `deck_workshop.js`, shared with the
  deck page). The open tab decides what "Make the deck" sends (`startBody`).
  - **A style**: a row of `DeckStyles()` in `styles.go` (plain, iron omens,
    hero's boons, tavern game, star reading, road events, ember curses), each
    drawn as a back and a face in its own look. Picking one only fills in the
    create request's `look`; `Plain` is the default look.
  - **Copy a deck**: the campaign's decks drawn as on the Decks page
    (`deckFace`), one choice. `POST /decks/from` with `deckId`.
  - **From a rolling table**: the tables as a plain list (name, "12 lines"),
    one choice, Director-only tables included. `POST /decks/from` with
    `tableId`.
  A tab with nothing to offer still shows, with "No decks to copy yet." or "No
  rolling tables yet."; without the rolling tables plugin the second reads the
  same and a create is refused ("Rolling tables are not available right now.").

### Starting a deck from a deck or a table

`Service.CreateFrom` (`create_from.go`; Directors only; the handler checks for
a Director before it reads the body) takes the typed name, who it is for and
exactly one of `deckId` or `tableId`, checks the name, kind, hero page and deck
limit as `Create` does (`checkNewDeck`), and stores the deck and every card with
its pile copies in one transaction (`Repository.CreateDeckWithCards`, all or
nothing, unique names and the 200-card and 600-copy limits enforced there). It
answers `{deck, report}`; `report.notes` are the sentences for what was left off.

- **Copy**: the source is read scoped to this campaign, so a deck of another
  campaign is the same 404 as a deck that is not there. Copied: the look (kinds
  and the back included), the rules except who draws (it goes back to
  only Directors, since the chosen players stay behind), and each card's name, kind, words, picture,
  corner, rare, copies, fate, ask first, Director's note and tests; every copy
  starts in the pile. Not copied: chosen players and draws left, the next and
  last card, "the Director says so" (`said_at`), anything in hands, on the
  table, used or kept, moments. Each test goes through `ValidateWhen` alone
  against the **new** deck (so a holder test follows the new hero); one it
  refuses is left off that card and counted, never stored. A picture that can
  no longer be used is left off and counted.
- **Table**: a new deck in the Plain look and the kind's default rules, one card
  per line: the line's name is the card's name, its brief the words, its weight
  the copies (rounded, at least 1, at most 20). A line with no name, a weight of
  0 or text a card cannot hold is skipped. A line named like an earlier one adds
  its copies to that card (card names are unique in a deck). Over the 200-card
  or 600-copy limit, the first lines that fit are made and the rest counted.
  Day conditions do not carry over (a card cannot yet wait for a day): such a
  line is still made, and the notes say "N lines only come up on certain days.
  Cards can't wait for a day yet, so those cards come up any time."
- **Tables** (`directory.go`): `List` and `Lines` over the campaign's rolling
  tables, implemented in `internal/app/decks_adapters.go` over
  `rolltables.Service` and bound only while that plugin is healthy
  (`WithTables`). `RollingTables` is the page's list; a reader that fails reads
  as none to offer.
- **After making**: the widget keeps `report.notes` in `sessionStorage` under
  the new deck's id and the deck's page shows them once under its heading
  (`.dkw-leftoff`, `role=status`), then forgets them, so they are gone on the
  next load. They are plain text, set with `textContent`.
- **A deck's page** (`/decks/workshop/:did`, `workshop_deck.templ`): the head
  (the name edits in place, saved as typed, in the page's display heading), the
  table row (`workshop_table.templ`: the pile with a draw stepper, the shown
  spot with its actions, the used pile, what is in play, and the pickers for
  the card that comes up next and the one kept for last), and four tabs. Each
  tab is also a fragment at `/decks/workshop/:did/{cards,look,rules,players}`,
  and the table row at `/decks/workshop/:did/table`; a full page and a fragment
  are the same markup.
  - **Cards** (`workshop_cards.templ`): the cards as a grid with copies, and
    one card's editor beside it (name, words, picture, kind, corner, rare,
    copies, where it goes after use, ask first, a note for Directors). Rare
    and ask-first are the standard's switch (`.toggle`). The editor is a
    container: with room for the card and its leaf beside a form at least 20rem
    wide it lays them side by side, otherwise the card sits above the form at
    row size without its leaf. A deck with no kinds shows "No kinds yet" and a
    link to the Look tab instead of empty chips.
  - **Look** (`workshop_look.go`, `workshop_look.templ`): a big card on a
    `data-dk-stage` with Play the turn-over, one row per choice drawn as small
    real cards (`lookRow`; a choice a shape cannot show is dimmed with its
    reason, and rows longer than eight fold behind Show all), the kinds editor,
    and the back's words. Each pick saves at once with the deck's version. A
    choice's picture is the real card at row size scaled down in a fixed box;
    a deck with no kinds draws its swatches with a sample kind and corner value
    (`swatchSample`) so the marker and corner rows differ. Starting from a style
    applies at once while the look still matches a style, and asks first once
    it has been changed (`data-changed` on the style note), naming the eleven
    choices it replaces and saying the kinds and the back's words stay. A kind's
    colour is a row of ten palette dots and its sign a row of eight glyph
    buttons, each a radio button named in words. A deck with no kinds shows
    "No kinds yet" with a link that opens the kinds editor.
  - **Rules** (`workshop_rules.go`): each setting is a sentence with its value
    as a blank (a `select.dw-blank`), one per key of `Settings`.
  - **Players** (`workshop_players.templ`): who may see the deck and how many
    draws each has left.
- Delete this deck is a button in a row of its own at the foot of the page.
- Every fragment (each tab and the table row) carries the deck's current
  version as `data-dkw-version`; the widget reads it from the fragment text
  (`parseVersion`), so a page whose save was refused as stale takes the new
  version from its reload and its next change saves. The same mark carries
  the deck's current name (`data-dkw-deckname`), so after a stale save the
  name field takes the newer name from the reload.
- On a phone the table row folds (the pile, stepper and Draw in one row, the
  shown cards in a strip that scrolls and snaps with 44px actions under each),
  the Look tab's card and Play the turn-over
  stay at the top while the choices scroll (the plate without its leaf, under
  45% of the screen), and every text and number field is 44 tall. On a touch
  screen or below 760 wide every control is a 44 by 44 tap target: a min-height
  (buttons, selects, the breadcrumb link, a check box through its row) or a
  hit area around a small switch; a mouse keeps the small size.
- "In hands" and "In play" list cards by holder: a line naming the holder and
  how many they hold, then that holder's cards in one row
  (`[data-dkw-strip]`) that scrolls inside itself on every width, each card
  with its note as a caption. The widget gives an overflowing row a tab stop
  and `data-more` while cards remain past its end, which the stylesheet fades.
- After Make the deck, the deck's page puts focus on its heading
  (`[data-dkw-heading]`, `tabindex="-1"`); the making page leaves the id in
  `sessionStorage` (`dkw-made`) and the deck page reads it once.
- Every write is the existing JSON route, sent by
  `static/js/deck_workshop.js` (widget `deck-workshop`). A move is sent first,
  then `DeckCards.move` plays it, then the table row is swapped from the
  server. A refused request says why in plain words and returns focus; a
  refused move (draw, deal, put back, shuffle and the like) first fetches the
  table row again so the pile's count, Draw and the stepper show what is true
  now; a stale version says the deck changed and reloads the tab and the row.
- Nothing here sends a face-down card's face to anyone but a Director: the pile
  and the back are `FaceDown()` and `CardBack`, and a card's id is Director data.
- Styles: `static/css/decks_workshop.css` (prefix `dkw-`, tokens only, amber for
  what only Directors see).

## The player's hand

A player's screens are drawn from the player types only (`PlayerDeck`,
`PlayerCopy`, `Face`, `TableCard`) through four service reads in `hand.go`;
no handler on a route a player can reach loads a Director type.

- **The Hand leaf** (`HandFragment`, `hand_panel.templ`): their own hand by hero,
  the party's hand and used pile, `Table` (face-up cards in play in front of
  nobody), other heroes' hands as `playerDeck` lets them be read (backs, or
  faces of shown copies), and decks open to them to draw from with the draws
  left. The Table drawer (rolls plugin, `table.js`) has the leaf when the
  add-on is on (`data-decks` on its host) and puts the fragment in it; a bell
  link `?table=hand` opens it.
- **A face-down card** is the deck's back from the look alone. Its markup holds
  the copy id (a copy in a hand the viewer may act on), the deck's name and the
  turn button, with an empty `data-face-src`; never a card id, name, words,

### The Director's side of asks

On a deck's table row, copies held in hands are listed ("In hands"), grouped by
holder, with whether each is still face down, turned over, or shown. A copy a player asked to play
says "<Hero> asks to play this" with Let them (`approve`) and Not now
(`turn-down`). A Director also gets a bell entry for an ask.

A waiting play is one ask, answered in one place (`asks.go`). Once the play
is stored as waiting, the service tells `Asks` (`WithAsks`, a `PlayAsk` with
the deck, card, copy, hero and its name, and the player), which
`internal/app/decks_homebrew.go` puts to the Homebrew Workshop as the
`card.play-asked` ask. So it is also the Homebrew drawer's ask card ("Tam
wants to play Pact from Omens", Yes, No, Other, and "Yes, and…" for the steps
rules on `card.play-asked` add) and the Foundry module's GM card. Let them and
Not now answer that same ask (`Asks.Answer`); whichever answer lands first
wins and the other is a 409, including while a drawer's Yes is still going
through (the losing drawer answer says so on the drawer's status line). The answer comes back through `AnswerAsk`, which
is the approve or turn-down itself, carrying an Other reply to the player's
bell (stored as the notification's detail). A play with no ask open (one asked before this way of asking, or while
the Workshop was away or failed to take it) is answered on the deck page as
before. Without `WithAsks` every play waits on the deck page alone.

A waiting card that leaves its wait without an answer closes its ask
(`Asks.Close`, with a line for the drawer): a Director's play, discard, take
back or keep of it, its player's own discard, a gather, and deleting its card
or deck. The close goes with a Director's standing, since it is the deck's own
bookkeeping. A Director turning the card leaves it waiting.

## Cards that wait for their moment

A card may carry up to four tests (`MaxTests`), all of which must hold; there are no formulas and no "any of". A test is one of `number`, `tracker`, `tag`, `layer`, `moon`, `event`, `time` or `say`, and is either **printed** on the card (`open: true`, part of the face anyone who can read the face reads) or the **Director's own** (never sent to a player, by any route). They are stored as a JSON list in `deck_cards.when_json`; `when_open` is derived (some test is printed) and `has_when` says the list is not empty.

| Test | Keys | Holds when |
|---|---|---|
| `number` | `who`, `field`, `op`, then `value` or `of` + `share` | the page's number (stored value, with its layers applied) compares to a value, or to a share (`quarter` ... `all`) of another number field |
| `tracker` | `who`, `kind`, `op`, `value` | the page has a tracker of that kind whose value compares; a kind not on the page does not hold |
| `tag` | `who`, `tag`, `has` | the page has (or has not) the tag, Director-only tags included |
| `layer` | `who`, `name`, `on` | a transformation whose name or preset source equals `name` (any case) is (or is not) on the page |
| `moon` | `name`, `is`, then `phase` or `from` + `to` | the main calendar's moon of that name (any case; it must exist when saved) is (or is not) in the phase today: a word of `Moon.PhaseWords` (`full`, `new`, `waxing`, `waning`, `crescent`, `gibbous`, `first-quarter`, `last-quarter`) or the phase's own name ("Waning Crescent", a hand-set moon's state); or on a day of its cycle from `from` to `to` (the new moon's day is 1, a range may wrap, 23 to 1; a hand-set moon has no days) |
| `event` | `name`, `has` | an event on the main calendar's current date has that name, or is of a kind with that slug or name (any case; kinds count only for a Director, since a player never learns them), or not; the name need not exist yet |
| `time` | `time`, `is` | the main calendar's time of day (`Calendar.DayPart`) is (or is not) `dawn`, `day`, `dusk` or `night` |
| `say` | none | the Director said so (`deck_cards.said_at`), cleared when a copy of the card leaves the pile |

`moon`, `event` and `time` read the calendar and take no `who`. Their matching is what worked-out sheet fields do (see `internal/plugins/fieldrules/.ai.md`, "What a rule follows"): the calendar read is shared in `internal/app/calendar_today.go`, and the moon matching (`moonMatches`, `fits.go`) is a copy of fieldrules', since plugins never import each other.

`who` is `holder` (the hero a hero deck is for), `any` (any hero of the party; every `any` test of one card must hold for the **same** hero, the first by name, which is the moment's subject) or a page id of the campaign. `op` is `lte`, `lt`, `gte`, `gt` or `eq`, compared with a tolerance so a third of 30 is exactly 10.

**One check.** `ValidateWhen` (`when.go`) is the only place a list of tests is checked; create, edit and (later) the AI call it. The request decoder (`WhenList`, `ParseWhen`) refuses an unknown kind or a key of another kind by name, so a typo is never stored as a test that quietly does nothing. A save is all or nothing and every message names the test (`test 2: ...`). Without `Facts` wired, only `say` can be saved. `BrokenTests` marks a stored test whose hero, field, tracker kind, tag or moon is gone; such a test can never hold. A calendar that cannot be read breaks nothing.

**One sentence writer.** `namer.sentence` says a test in words ("Tam's Hit points is at or below half of Max hit points"); the editor, the moment's notice and the card face all use it. `printedLine` joins the printed tests as `When A and B.`, per viewer: a hero is named to a player only if they may open its page, otherwise "a page", and a moon only if their calendar shows it, otherwise "a moon". An event's name is the Director's own typed words and is printed as written, like a layer's. It never carries a Director's own test.

**Where facts come from.** `Facts` (`facts.go`) is declared here and implemented in `internal/app/decks_facts.go` over the entities, tags, trackers, transformations and calendar services, so decks imports no other plugin. Every method answers as a Director would see it, except `Today` (the main calendar's current date: the moons, today's events and their kinds, the time of day), which is read as a named viewer: the Director for a Director's draw, a moment and the editor, the player for a player's own draw. A campaign with no main calendar, no current date, or the calendar plugin degraded is `ErrUnavailable`, so every calendar test (and its "not" form) does not hold. A fact that cannot be read (error, plugin degraded, page gone or in another campaign) is a **missing fact**, and a test on a missing fact does not hold, so a failing source can never make a card come up; `ErrUnavailable` is the quiet form (no log line). `Numbers` reads the stored sheet (`fields_data`) of the page's number fields and applies its layers in order: `set` replaces, `add` adds to a value that exists, an `add` on a missing value stays missing, `append` and `remove` are not numbers and are ignored. A page with no stored sheet never satisfies a number test. With the transformations plugin degraded, `Numbers` is unavailable (a layer might have changed the number), so number tests do not hold.

**What the server cannot see.** A number the sheet only works out in the browser (a formula or rule field) is not stored, so a test on it reads as missing. A stored value does not say whether a hand or Foundry wrote it, so a reading's source is "layer" only where a layer set or changed it.

### What a draw does with tests

- A Director's draw and "draw another" pick among cards that fit now (a card with no test always fits). Nothing fitting is a 409 ("No card in the pile fits right now. You can still deal a card by name.") and **nothing is reshuffled**: a reshuffle is for a pile that is truly empty, judged on the raw pile before the fit filter (`errNoneFit` in the repository).
- Deal by name ignores conditions: it is the Director's own decision.
- A player's own draw only pulls a card with no test, or whose tests are all printed and all hold. A card with any Director-only test is never in the set, whether or not it holds, and its facts are not even read. When nothing is eligible the reply is the empty-pile reply, byte for byte (`errNothingLeft`), so it cannot say a hidden test is why. A printed test about a page the player may not open also keeps the card out (`pagesOpenTo`), since drawing it would say something about a page they cannot read. The calendar tests are judged on the calendar as that player sees it: a moon hidden from them is a missing fact, so the card stays out whatever the moon does, and an event hidden from them is not today. What they draw never depends on a hidden moon or event. A draw that loses a race for the moment's card answers the same empty-pile reply.
- An invited draw (below) picks among cards that have tests and fit, spends no draw, and closes the moment as `drawn`.

### Moments

A moment is one waiting notice for one deck: the card the deck picked, blind and weighted by copies in the pile, among the cards that fit. It is stored in `deck_moments` with `open_deck_id` set to the deck id while it is open (`waiting` or `invited`) and NULL once closed, so a unique key allows one open moment per deck. Nothing leaves the pile until a Director answers; Chronicle never deals on its own.

`Recheck(campaign)` (`moments.go`) first asks one indexed question (`WatchedDecks`: decks with a card that has tests and a notice that is not `quiet`, plus decks with an open moment). Then per deck: judge the cards that have tests and a copy in the pile; set `fits_since` on cards that started to fit and clear it on cards that stopped; a moment is raised only on the **rising edge** (some card newly started to fit), so a card that keeps holding raises nothing again after "Not now". An open moment stays while its card fits, is picked again while another card fits, and lapses when none does. An invited moment stays while any card fits. A moment closing within 5 minutes of the last one is `quiet`.

Recheck does nothing while the card decks add-on is off for the campaign (`WithSwitch`, bound in `internal/app/routes.go`), so a switched-off deck raises no notice. Two rechecks may run at once: `SetFits` answers how many cards it really marked as started, and only the one that marked some raises a moment; `Refresh` changes a moment only while it is still on the card it was read with. A reading is cut to 500 characters before it is stored. **Known gap:** a card that began to fit while another card's moment was open keeps its `fits_since`, so after that moment is answered it raises none of its own until it stops fitting and starts again; the Director still finds it with Draw another, or by drawing.

Answers (all Director-only, 404 for anyone else): **Deal** (an ordinary deal of the card; the moment is claimed inside the same transaction as the draw, `DrawPlan.Moment`, so two Directors cannot both answer: the loser is a 409 "already answered"), **Draw another** (a re-pick among the other fitting cards), **Let them draw** (invites the claimed player of the hero the card would go to: a bell entry `deck_draw_invited`, riding on the card-decks choice, and a live `decks.changed` to that player only), **Not now** (dismiss, also takes an invitation back). Deleting a card an invited player was asked to draw tells that player's Hand to look again, so the invitation does not linger.

Who learns what: that a moment came, which card, whether a hidden test holds and what was read are Directors-only, by every channel. `decks.moment` (a websocket message, `RequiresDM`, no payload) tells Directors to fetch again. The one thing a player can learn is that they were invited to draw, and only the invited player learns it.

### The editor and the notice

**Comes up when (Cards tab).** The card editor's `section[data-dkw-when]` is rendered by `workshop_cards.templ` with the card's tests (`data-when`, `WhenView` with its sentence and `broken`) and its "Now" state (`data-state`: `fitsNow`, `whyNot`, `saidAt`). The rows are built in the browser by `deck_workshop.js` from `GET /decks/when-options` (`heroes`, `fields`, `trackers`, `tags`, `layers`, `hasCalendar`, `moons`, `eventKinds`, `ops`, `shares`, `testKinds`, `dayParts`, `moonPhases`, `maxTests`), so the choices are always the ones the server accepts; `test/js/deck_workshop.test.mjs` pins `TEST_KEYS` and `MAX_TESTS` to `when.go`. A row is a mark ("On the card" / "Only you", a button), the sentence with native `select.dw-blank` blanks and typed numbers, and a remove button. Each change sends the whole list in one card update (`PUT /decks/:did/cards/:cid`, `when`) and the server's check is the only check: a refusal is said in the row in the server's words and the row is redrawn from what is stored; a stale save reloads. A kind the campaign has nothing for is left off the add menu (a moon test needs a moon, an event or time test a main calendar), and a layer or event test with no name yet is a draft that is not sent. A calendar row has no "whose page" blank: a moon row is the moon, is or is not, and a phase or "on certain days of its cycle" with two whole-number day blanks; an event row is a typed name (the kinds of event as suggestions) and "is today" or "is not today"; a time row is "it", is or is not, and the day part. The preview leaf prints the printed tests and a hint ("The second condition is yours alone, so the card shows only the first.") when some are the Director's own; the grid marks "Fits now" or "Waiting". Say so / You said so. / Take it back is the `say` route.

**The printed line.** `Face.When` is added once (`printedFrom` in `workshop.go` for the Director, `printedLine` for a player) and drawn by the leaf and the compact card as `.dk-when`, so every place a face is read carries it. On a phone the editor's preview hides the leaf (existing rule), so the line is read in the rows there.

**The notice.** `GET /decks/moments/panel[?at=<id>]` (`moment_panel.go`, `moment_panel.templ`) is a server-rendered fragment for the Table: a hidden index of every open moment (`li[data-id data-slide data-deck data-reading]`) and the one shown (reading, the card in the deck's look, the answers in order: Deal to X, Draw another, Let X draw, Not now; an answer the moment cannot take is not drawn, and a 409 is said in a line). The handler checks for a Director first, so everyone else is told 404 before anything is read. Only a Director's Table (`data-director` and `data-decks`) ever fetches it, at load and on the window event `chronicle:decks-moment` (from the Directors-only, payload-free `decks.moment` message). `table.js` (rolls plugin) turns the index into the count and the drawer's "Waiting on you" rows, slides the notice in when a new moment comes that is not quiet and the deck's `notice` setting is `slide`, and keeps it until it is answered or closed. See `internal/plugins/rolls/.ai.md`.

**The invited player.** A player invited to draw is told through the existing `PlayerDeck.InvitedHeroID` (set only for the invited user, for their own hero): the Hand leaf shows "Your Director invites you to draw." with the Draw control and no draws-left count. Nothing else of the moment reaches a player.

### What wakes a recheck

A watcher in `internal/app/decks_watcher.go` coalesces `Poke(campaign)` per campaign (about 300ms later, its own 10s context; a poke during a recheck queues one more). Pokes come from: the entity event publisher (`created`, `updated`, `deleted`, and a category change), the tags hook dispatcher (a tag put on or taken off), the transformations notifier (a layer added or removed), a trackers change observer (`SetChangeObserver`: any log line, strike, place, patch, remove or kind change or deletion, for every kind), the calendar event publisher (`decksCalendarEvents`: the date or time moved, an event made, changed or removed, the calendar's structure or moons changed, a moon's phase moved; not weather), and decks itself (card edits, say, moves). The watcher is bound only when the decks plugin is healthy. A Director's own read of `GET /decks/moments` looks again first, at most once per 5 seconds per campaign, which catches a change no hook saw.

**Known gaps (no hook fires, so the next Director read or poke catches it):** a tag deleted outright (its pages lose it without a per-page hook), a page cloned with its tags, a tracker kind's bounds edited by a path other than `UpdateKind`, a page deleted through a path that publishes no event, a calendar that tracks real time (its date and time of day move with the clock, unannounced), and a hand-set moon's new mark. A test also never *starts* fitting while nobody is looking; the 5-second read is the backstop.

## What the AI can see and change

The AI part is `ai.go` (`AI`, `NewAI`, the `deck` kind), `ai_card.go` (the `card` kind)
and `ai_lookup.go` (the `decks` lookup, tool `card_decks`), all against
`internal/aiplug` (ADR-071). Decks imports no other plugin: `internal/app` hands
`NewAI` a page finder and the add-on gate (`DecksAI`).

| Key | Sees | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Owner (the Director) | `List`, `Get` (rules, cards, tests with whether each fits, where each copy is, next and last) and `Moments` | `deck` and `card` blocks: create and update; `deck` also takes `action: delete` (a whole deck, never a single card); no draws, moves or players |
| A player's own key | `Hand` only: the cards in their own hand, a face-down card as "a face-down card from <deck>" | nothing here |
| The owner's what-players-see key | `Table` as a viewer that owns no hero (`tableViewerID`): cards lying face up on the table | nothing here |

Rules the code keeps:

- Both kinds are Director only and refused when the add-on is off. `Apply`
  re-plans, then writes through `Service` with a Director `Viewer`; it never
  touches the repository.
- Updates are partial: only a key present in the block changes; `rename_to`
  renames. A card's `when` goes through `ParseWhen` and `ValidateWhen`, and the
  plan summary uses the editor's sentence writer (`SayWhen`).
- A card may name a deck created by an earlier row of the same paste (`Staged`).
- A deck `delete` takes the name and nothing else (another key is refused by
  name), runs behind the same gate as every other row, and plans with the
  deck's card count and how many copies are in hands or on the table, plus the
  workshop's own warning and "This cannot be undone." `Apply` re-plans and calls
  `Service.Delete`, so a deck that is already gone is refused. `Stage` notes
  nothing for a delete, so a later `card` row naming that deck plans cleanly
  from the stored decks but is refused when applied. The plan's `Seen` is a
  hash of the deck's id, version and zone counts as reviewed; `Apply` deletes
  only when the record's `Seen` matches a fresh plan's, so a deck that gained
  cards or had a copy move since review, or a record with no `Seen`, is
  refused. The `card` kind never
  deletes.
- `Record.Fields` is the whole front matter, so a card's own kind and the
  card's place in a deck use `card_kind` and `name_position` rather than the
  reserved `kind` and `name`.
- A Director's read of one deck lists `cardsPerAnswer` cards at a time and
  says which `from` to ask for next, so a long deck is never cut short without
  saying so; where the copies are is said in full every time.
- A deck update that changes who the deck is for, or `knows` or `others_see`,
  carries a warning on the review line that sight changes.
- A hidden test and a moment never reach a player's or the table key. The
  sight tests (`ai_sight_test.go`) build the same campaign four ways (a hidden
  test holding or not, a moment open or dismissed, the other card face down) and
  require each player's and the table key's answer to hold the same lines.

## In the story

Decks tells the Homebrew Workshop what happened in a deck, and a rule can end in
a deck action. Decks imports no other plugin: `story.go` declares `Story`
(`CardDrawn`, `CardPlayed`, each with a `CardEvent`: deck and card ids and
names, the hero page the card is with, "" for the table or the party, and who
made the move), set by `WithStory` and nil when not wired. `internal/app/decks_homebrew.go`
implements it over the Workshop and registers the events and actions.

- **`card.played`** fires after a card is really played: a player's or a
  Director's play, and a Director's Yes to an "ask first" card's ask, from
  the drawer or Let them (the ask itself fires nothing, nor does a
  turn-down). The hero is the one whose hand it left.
- **`card.play-asked`** is the ask a player's play of an "ask first" card puts
  to the Director (see "The Director's side of asks"), before the play
  happens, with the hero's page as scope. It is never fired: a rule on it adds
  its steps after the ask, to run on Yes, and the Director may change them
  while answering ("Yes, and…"). Its words name the hero, card and deck to a
  Director, and to the asker only while the deck is listed to them ("play a
  card" otherwise).
- **`card.drawn`** fires once per card that arrives where people play it
  from: a hero's hand, the party's hand or the table. That is a deal from the
  shown spot, a deal by name, a deal from a moment, a player's own draw and an
  invited draw, with the hero's page as scope when it went to a hero. It does
  not fire for a Director's draw to the shown spot, for `another` or for a put
  back: those are the Director looking, private everywhere else in decks, so a
  card looked at and then dealt fires exactly once, on the deal.
- Both fire after the move's transaction has committed and the deck's lock is
  released. The adapter runs each on its own goroutine and short context,
  checks that the campaign has the add-on on, and logs a failure; a panic in a
  listener or a rule is caught (`tell`, and again in the adapter), so a rule can
  never fail or slow a move.
- Each event takes optional `deck` and `card` names; a Director can say "when
  Second wind is played" or "when any card is drawn from Omens". A player's move
  starts the Director's rule with a Director's standing, with the hero's page
  as scope, so a tracker action lands on that hero's page. A card with no hero
  (played from the party's hand, or dealt to the table) has no scope, and a
  tracker action then changes that tracker on **every page that has it**, as
  any Workshop event from no page does.
- A rule these events would hold is held once (`Fired.Once`): while it waits
  for the same hero and card, the same card played or drawn again adds no
  second copy to the drawer, since a player can do that as often as they like.
- **Actions**, all of which wait for a Director to press Run: `deck.add-copies`
  (a card's copies raised through the card edit, 1 to the per-card limit),
  `deck.take-out` ("Take a card out of a deck for good") and `deck.shuffle-back`
  (`Shuffle`: only the used pile goes back into the pile, so no rule can empty a
  hand or the table). Take-out has two cases, and the result line says which:
  when some copies are held elsewhere it removes exactly the pile's copies and
  leaves every copy in a hand, in play, in the shown spot or used where it is;
  when the pile held every copy the card itself leaves the deck, because a card
  keeps at least one copy. Known limits, all from reading the copies and then
  writing: a deal landing in between can make take-out's lowering remove a used
  copy instead of the one just dealt, or, where the pile held every copy, take
  the card out of the hand it was just dealt to; and a copy kept into the deck
  in between leaves add-copies one short of the total its sentence says. Each
  needs a Director pressing Run in the same instant as a deal. Actions resolve deck and card by name
  as the Director who pressed Run, refuse anyone else, and fail with a plain
  sentence for a name that matches none or more than one. No action deals, draws
  or plays a card: a rule cannot move a card to a person.
- **What a rule may reveal.** A rule is the Director's own: if it adds to a
  tracker players see when a face-down card is drawn, the players can work out
  which card it was. That is the Director's choice. The event itself never
  reaches a browser: decks sends no message, bell entry or reply because a
  listener exists, and a held rule shows only in the Directors' drawer
  (`TestTwoWorldsAStoryListenerChangesNoReplyAndNoAudience`, and in
  `internal/app` `TestAPlayersFaceDownDrawIsTheSameWithARuleListeningOrNot`).

## Leak-hardening rules

- A copy that returns to the pile (take back, put back a shown card, reshuffle on an empty pile, shuffle, gather) gets a fresh id, so an id a player once saw cannot be followed back through the pile. `ActionResult.CopyID` is empty for a move to the pile. The log keeps `card_name` so its lines still read by card.
- A player's kind counts include every copy whose face the player cannot see: the pile, shown cards, used and gone cards of a deck that is not a party deck, and other heroes' cards unless visible to them.
- A player is sent a kind's name, colour and sign only for a face they can read, unless the deck lets them know kinds (`knows` of `kinds` or `faces`): `kindsKnown` trims the look in `playerDeck`, and `Table` trims each deck's look to the kinds of its table cards.
- Copy lists in player replies are ordered by arrival (`moved_at`), then id, never by card. `Move` sets `moved_at` only when the zone or holder changes, so a turn, a show, an ask and its answer keep a card's place and another player cannot read a private act from the order.
- A player turns a card face up only; a repeat answers 409 and writes no log line or notice. A Director may turn it back. A player may play only a face-up card. A repeat show answers 409 the same way.
- Turning never changes what another player is sent (`TestTurningIsPrivateAndShowingIsASecondStep` compares the bytes); only a show does.
- A Director's draws, put-backs and the next and last cards change nothing a player reads (`TestTwoWorldsNudgesAndLookedAtCardsAreInvisibleToPlayers`); a copy in the shown spot still counts in the pile a player is told, so the count falls only when a card reaches a hand, the table or the used pile.
- The five move routes carry a per-user limit of 120 a minute (`middleware.UserRateLimit`).
- Saving a look refuses an unknown key with a message naming it; reading a stored look falls back field by field, validates kinds one at a time and re-checks back words.
- A `Notifier` message carries nothing, and a bell entry to a player may name only the deck (and the card only if it was face up to them); the audience is a function of the move and the deck's settings, so a hidden card or a Director's private act cannot change who hears.
- A player's fragment holds a copy id only for a copy in a hand they may act on, and no card id at all; the fragment bytes are the same whichever card lies face down (`TestTwoWorldsTheHandFragmentIsTheSameBytes`).
- The turn route carries the same per-user limit as the move routes; the open and turn routes answer 404 with one body for anything hidden.
- A Director's own test is never sent to a player and never read for one: a player's draw does not even ask the facts about a card that has one, and a player's reply when nothing is eligible is the empty-pile reply byte for byte (`TestAPlayersNothingEligibleReplyIsTheEmptyPileReply`, `TestTwoWorldsAHiddenTestHoldingIsInvisibleToPlayers`).
- The moment routes and `when-options` ask for a Director before reading anything and answer 404 in the workshop's words; `decks.moment` is `RequiresDM` with no payload; only the invited player is told of an invitation, with the same payload-free `decks.changed` every player gets.
