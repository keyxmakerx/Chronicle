# Rolls Plugin

## Purpose

The campaign's **roll log** and the header's **Table tab** (`data-widget="table"`
in `layouts/app.templ`, on every campaign page for members). A roll made at the
Table, reported by Foundry, or made by another plugin on the server (crafting's
project roll) is saved here and sent live as `roll.made` to the people its mode
lets see it. Built against the signed Table tab mockup (`.ai/ui-standard.md`,
"The table is one tab away"; Chronicle#1475).

## Who sees what

| Mode | Directors | The roller | Everyone else |
|---|---|---|---|
| `public` | yes | yes | yes |
| `gm` | yes | yes | no |
| `blind` | yes | a stub: no dice, modifier, total or outcome | no |
| `self` | no | yes | no |

A Director is the owner or a member with DM access (`VisibilityRole` at
Owner). A Director's blind roll is a `gm` roll. The same rule is written three
times on purpose, so one slip can't leak a roll: `visibleTo` in the
repository's SQL, `visible`/`viewOf` in the service, and `publish`'s audiences,
which the hub's `Message.DeliversTo` enforces per socket
(`internal/app/rolls_adapters.go`; pinned by `TestRollsPublisherAudience`).

## Who decides the numbers

- **Table rolls:** the server, from `crypto/rand` (`dice.go`). The browser sends
  a formula (`2d10+2`, `d20`, `4d6-1`; dice 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 20, 100; at
  most 50 dice) and plays what comes back. 30 rolls a minute per person.
- **Foundry rolls:** Foundry. `POST /api/v1/campaigns/:id/rolls`
  (`syncapi/rolls_api.go`) takes them from a stored Bearer key only, never a
  signed-in browser. The Foundry user is linked to a member through the
  module's own player report (`foundry_players`). The unique key on
  `(campaign_id, origin, origin_id)` makes a replay a no-op (200,
  `duplicate: true`). Self rolls stay in Foundry.
- **Other plugins:** they call `Service.Log` through an adapter (crafting:
  `crafting.RollLog`). Only dice, total and progress travel, never crafting's
  terms, whose words can carry a Director's secret rule.

Rolls are not in the sync change feed: a reconnect never replays old rolls into
Foundry chat; the log is read through `GET /campaigns/:id/rolls`.

## Routes

| Route | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /campaigns/:id/rolls?before=&limit=` | members | a page of the log as the viewer may see it, oldest first, with `more` |
| `POST /campaigns/:id/rolls` | members | `{formula, label, mode}`: roll at the Table |
| `GET /campaigns/:id/rolls/settings` | members | the table's dice look |
| `PUT /campaigns/:id/rolls/settings` | Directors (service-checked) | `{look: quick\|rune\|none}` |
| `POST /api/v1/campaigns/:id/rolls` | the Foundry module's key | a roll made in Foundry |

## The Table tab

- Leaves side by side like the DM Screen, one per section; picking a section
  another leaf shows swaps the two. Each person's layout and dice look are
  remembered in their browser (`chronicle.table.*`). On a phone one leaf shows
  behind a switch.
- A roll plays in the viewer's own look: quick 2D (`table_dice.js`), rune 3D
  (`Chronicle.dice`, with the server's values; dice it doesn't come in play as
  quick 2D) or numbers only. A new player starts on the table's look, which a
  Director sets in My dice. A hidden roll throws nothing.
- When the dice land the island slides out from behind the tab, then back in,
  and the tab's count grows; while the log is showing, the row glows instead.
  Clicking the island or a row shows the dice, the math, the outcome, why,
  where from and who sees it. New rolls queue while the island's details are
  open.
- T toggles the Table, R opens it on the roller; never while typing. Escape
  closes a leaf menu, then the details, then the Table. Calm and Off motion are
  honoured.
