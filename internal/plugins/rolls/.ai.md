# Rolls Plugin

## Purpose

The campaign's **roll log** and the header's **Table tab** (`data-widget="table"`
in `layouts/app.templ`, on every campaign page for members). A roll made at the
Table, reported by Foundry, or made by another plugin on the server (crafting's
project roll) is saved here and sent live as `roll.made` to the people its mode
lets see it. Built against the signed Table tab mockup (`.ai/ui-standard.md`,
"The table is one tab away"; Chronicle#1475).

## Who sees what

| Mode | Directors | The roller | Everyone else |
|---|---|---|---|
| `public` | yes | yes | yes |
| `gm` | yes | yes | no |
| `blind` | yes | a stub: no dice, modifier, total or outcome | no |
| `self` | no | yes | no |

A Director is the owner or a member with DM access (`VisibilityRole` at
Owner). A Director's blind roll is a `gm` roll. The same rule is written three
times on purpose, so one slip can't leak a roll: `visibleTo` in the
repository's SQL, `visible`/`viewOf` in the service, and `publish`'s audiences,
which the hub's `Message.DeliversTo` enforces per socket
(`internal/app/rolls_adapters.go`; pinned by `TestRollsPublisherAudience`).

## Who decides the numbers

- **Table rolls:** the server, from `crypto/rand` (`dice.go`). The browser sends
  a formula (`2d10+2`, `d20`, `4d6-1`; dice 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 20, 100; at
  most 50 dice) and plays what comes back. 30 rolls a minute per person.
- **A hero's roll:** a Table roll may carry `characterId`, the hero whose
  numbers it uses (the Draw Steel sheet's characteristic rolls, and the
  Table's power roll line when it shows the viewer's hero). The service asks
  `CharacterSource` (`rollsCharacterAdapter` in `internal/app`) whether the
  viewer may: a live page of this campaign that the viewer has claimed, or
  any of them for a Director, because Foundry posts the roll as that hero's
  actor; a public roll also needs a page every player may see (not private,
  default visibility), since Foundry shows the actor to the whole table.
  Otherwise, or when the check fails, the roll is still made, under the
  roller's name alone. The id rides
  only on the live `roll.made` and the roller's reply; the log does not keep
  it.
- **Foundry rolls:** Foundry. `POST /api/v1/campaigns/:id/rolls`
  (`syncapi/rolls_api.go`) takes them from a stored Bearer key only, never a
  signed-in browser. The Foundry user is linked to a member through the
  module's own player report (`foundry_players`). The unique key on
  `(campaign_id, origin, origin_id)` makes a replay a no-op (200,
  `duplicate: true`). Self rolls stay in Foundry.
- **Other plugins:** they call `Service.Log` through an adapter (crafting:
  `crafting.RollLog`). Only dice, total and progress travel, never crafting's
  terms, whose words can carry a Director's secret rule.

- **Crits:** the server judges them, under the campaign's game system, on every
  path (Table, Foundry report, other plugins' `Log`/`Record`) and stores the
  verdict on each die (`Die.Crit`: `succ`, `fail` or empty). A crit a client
  sends is overwritten. The rules are the system manifest's `crits` block
  (`internal/systems/crits.go`; default: natural 20 and 1 on a d20), read
  through the optional `CritSource` that `internal/app/rolls_adapters.go`
  implements; `crits.go` here holds `markCrits`. A Table roll is grouped by the
  formula's terms; a reported roll has none, so same-size runs stand in.

- **Whose dice:** every roll carries `rollerDice` (`{set, colour, critSucc,
  critFail, own}`), the 3D dice every screen throws it in, settled by the
  server when the roll is made, on every path (Table, Foundry report, other
  plugins' `Log`/`Record`). It is the roller's own pick laid over the
  campaign's dice field by field (`effectiveDice` in `service.go`) while the
  campaign lets players pick; the pick's set counts only while it is on offer,
  and its colour goes with it. A pick that no longer counts is skipped, never
  deleted, so offering the set again brings it back. A roll with no known
  roller (a Foundry user nobody linked) carries the campaign's dice, `own`
  false. It is stored with the roll (`roller_dice`), sent on `roll.made`, the
  roller's reply and the log; a blind roll's stub carries none, and a roll from
  before rolls carried it has none, so screens throw it in the campaign's dice.
  Old clients ignore the field.
- **A member's own dice** are kept with their account per campaign
  (`campaign_roll_member_dice`), read and replaced through
  `GET/PUT /campaigns/:id/rolls/my-dice`. The service checks a pick against
  the settings: players must be let pick, the set must be one on offer, every
  name one the engine draws. The empty pick (back to the campaign's) is always
  allowed. Only the caller's own row is ever written. A Director picks the
  same way; the campaign's dice themselves change only through the settings.

Rolls are not in the sync change feed: a reconnect never replays old rolls into
Foundry chat; the log is read through `GET /campaigns/:id/rolls`.

## Routes

| Route | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /campaigns/:id/rolls?before=&limit=` | members | a page of the log as the viewer may see it, oldest first, with `more` |
| `POST /campaigns/:id/rolls` | members | `{formula, label, mode, characterId?}`: roll at the Table, as the viewer's own hero when `characterId` is set |
| `GET /campaigns/:id/rolls/settings` | members | the campaign's dice: `{look, diceSet, diceColour, critSucc, critFail, quality, playersPick, diceOffered}` |
| `GET /campaigns/:id/rolls/my-dice` | members | the caller's own dice: `{pick: {set, colour, critSucc, critFail}, effective: {set, colour, critSucc, critFail, own}, mayPick}` |
| `PUT /campaigns/:id/rolls/my-dice` | members (service-checked) | `{set, colour, critSucc, critFail}` replaces the caller's pick (empty = the campaign's); refused while players may not pick, or for a set not on offer |
| `PUT /campaigns/:id/rolls/settings` | Directors (service-checked) | a partial change to those same keys (a key left out keeps what is stored; `look` is `quick\|rune\|none`, `quality` is `auto\|basic\|hd`, `diceOffered` replaces the whole list and an empty one offers every set) |
| `GET /campaigns/:id/rolls/power` | members | the game system's power roll (`declared`, dice, edge, bane, tiers, characteristics) and the viewer's own claimed hero's name, page id (`heroId`) and numbers, with GM-only fields left out unless the viewer sees GM content; `{declared:false}` when the system declares none |
| `POST /api/v1/campaigns/:id/rolls` | the Foundry module's key | a roll made in Foundry |

## Customize › Dice

- The campaign's die: each set in the dice catalogue is a tile with a picture of
  its own d20 (`Chronicle.dice.portrait`, drawn once and kept; a flat d20 in the
  set's colours where there is no WebGL) and its name. The tiles are one radio
  group, so arrow keys move the pick; the chosen tile has the accent ring. The
  grid takes however many sets the catalogue lists. Under it, the chosen set's
  note, then its colourways as swatches with the chosen colour's note.
- The tray sits beside the tiles on a wide screen and stays in view while the
  page scrolls (below the Customize tab row); on a narrow screen it folds in
  under the colours. It shows the chosen die at rest, a label with the set and
  colour, and three quiet words: Roll again, Crit and Fumble. Picking a set or
  colour, or pressing a word, throws the die inside the tray (`opts.host`) in the
  campaign's look, where it stays resting; Calm sets it down with a fade; Off, a
  reduced-motion device or no WebGL show it at rest on the number thrown. The
  number is read out to screen readers.
- Below: Detail, Crit success, Crit fail, Players (and the sets on offer), and
  the table's dice look. Every pick saves at once with a partial
  `PUT /campaigns/:id/rolls/settings`, shows "Saved", and is put back with the
  server's reason if refused. The tray is drawn once; the rest of the tab is
  redrawn after each pick.
- Players: "Players may pick their own dice" and, while it is on, the sets on
  offer to players. The campaign's own set always stays on offer. With it on,
  each player's rolls are thrown in their own pick on everyone's screen; off,
  every roll is thrown in the campaign's dice. Only a Director changes any of
  it (`SetSettings` refuses anyone else, `TestOnlyDirectorsSetTheCampaignDice`).
- Players' own picks live in the Table's My dice leaf (`table.js`), a separate
  picker that saves to their account.

## The Table tab

- **A card's moment (Card decks add-on, Directors only).** The tab's count has two sources: rolls not yet seen (cleared by looking at the log) and the open moments the server reports (cleared only when they are answered or lapse). The host's `data-director` and `data-decks` must both be true for the Table to fetch `GET /campaigns/:id/decks/moments/panel`, which it does at load and on the window event `chronicle:decks-moment` (raised from the Directors-only `decks.moment` socket message, which carries nothing); a player's Table never asks. The fragment's hidden index gives the count and the drawer's first line, "Waiting on you"; a moment that comes while the page is open and is not quiet, in a deck whose notice setting is slide, mounts `#tt-moment` (under the tab on a wide screen, the top of the sheet on a phone). It takes no focus when it slides in (said once in the live region), unless the drawer is open: the drawer already lists the moment and a notice hung beside it would cover that list, so none is hung. It stays until answered or closed; closing it, by its button, Escape or a press outside, keeps the count. In the phone's sheet the Table's own close mark is the only one on screen (the notice's is hidden there); Escape from the notice's heading still puts it away. One the Director opens from "Waiting on you" puts focus on its heading, and closing returns it to the Table tab. Motion follows the island: Full slides, Calm fades, Off is the end state. The fragment is built by the decks plugin (see its `.ai.md`); `test/js/table_moments_browser.test.mjs` pins the count and the no-request rule.
- Leaves side by side like the DM Screen, one per section; picking a section
  another leaf shows swaps the two. Each person's layout and dice look are
  remembered in their browser (`chronicle.table.*`); their own rune dice are
  kept with their account (a pick an older Table kept in the browser moves to
  the account on first load, then the browser's copy is dropped). On a phone one leaf shows
  behind a switch. At the sheet's lifted height the switch's three names
  (Rolls, Tonight, My dice) fill the otherwise empty top row, laid over it so
  the dice and roll field stay put; a tap on one raises the sheet to full
  height on that leaf. Players get the same.
- A roll plays in the viewer's own look: quick 2D (`table_dice.js`), rune 3D
  (`Chronicle.dice`, with the server's values, in the roll's `rollerDice`, so
  everyone sees a player's roll in that player's dice; dice it doesn't come in
  play as quick 2D) or numbers only. A roll's details name the dice
  ("Mira’s Clockwork · Brass", "The campaign’s Neon · Blue"). Each die carries the server's crit: quick 2D shows
  it as a static coloured edge, rune 3D plays the crit animation. Each person's
  "Crit animations" On/Off in My dice (`chronicle.table.crits.*`, default on)
  turns the effects off for their rune throws and, through
  `Chronicle.diceTable.crits === false`, their other 3D rolls on the page; the
  marks stay. A new player starts on the table's look, which a
  Director sets in My dice. A hidden roll throws nothing.
- When the dice land the island slides out from behind the tab, then back in,
  and the tab's count grows; while the log is showing, the row glows instead.
  Clicking the island or a row shows the dice, the math, the outcome, why,
  where from and who sees it. New rolls queue while the island's details are
  open.
- **Power roll line** (Draw Steel and any system whose `crafting` block names
  characteristics and rolls 2d10; `SystemManifest.PowerRoll`): at the top of the
  roller, the hero's characteristics as one-press chips (from the viewer's own
  claimed character via `rollsPowerAdapter`; a person with no hero numbers types
  a bonus), a five-step double bane to double edge control that clears after the
  roll, and the quick dice become d10, d6, d3 with the rest behind "Other dice".
  The server still rolls a plain formula; the label carries the characteristic
  and the edge ("Might, edge"), so any viewer works out the tier from the stored
  dice, total and label (`power_roll.js`). A single edge or bane is the system's
  +2 / -2 in the formula; a double one moves the tier a step instead. A natural
  19 or 20 is tier 3. The result reads "Tier 2 · 14" (and "Critical · Tier 3 ·
  20" when a die's `crit` is `succ`, set by the server) in the notice, the log
  and the details. A system with no declaration leaves the Table as it was.
- T toggles the Table, R opens it on the roller; never while typing. Escape
  closes a leaf menu, then the details, then the Table. Calm and Off motion are
  honoured.
- Tonight lists what the Director showed from a scene (the encounters
  plugin's `table.shown` message and `GET /campaigns/:id/table/shown`): a
  note's words or a link to the page, newest last. Something shown while
  Tonight isn't on screen slides out the island ("The Director shows: …"),
  which opens to the same note or link.
