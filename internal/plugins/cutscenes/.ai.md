# Cut scenes Plugin

## Purpose

Short full-screen moments the Director shows chosen players: the screen goes
dark, something appears, a few words. Behind the **Cut scenes** add-on
(`cutscenes`, off until a campaign turns it on). Tables, repository, service,
the Director's pages (list, editor, Show sheet), the players' JSON routes, the
live hub messages and the AI part. Registered in `internal/app/routes.go`
behind the plugin's health gate; migrations are the plugin's own
(`migrations/`), new tables only.

The engine that draws a scene (`static/js/cutscene_engine.js`,
`window.ChronicleCutscene.play(root, scene, {onClose})`) and the stage that
plays showings for players (`static/js/widgets/cutscene_stage.js`) are core
static files, not this plugin's. `engine_kinds_test.go` fails when the server
accepts a kind the engine never names.

## A scene

Stored as one row in `cutscenes`, with each layer a JSON column. The service
normalises every write through `Normalize` (`validate.go`); the stored text is
a re-marshal of the typed struct, so unknown keys never survive.

```json
{
  "id": "…", "name": "The Eye Opens",
  "background": {"kind": "fall-asleep", "seconds": 7, "params": {"look": "ink"}},
  "overlays": [{"kind": "eye", "at": 7.5, "seconds": 3.5, "params": {"color": "#3dff7a", "look": "rift"}}],
  "text": [{"say": "Something has noticed you.", "at": 11, "seconds": 5, "style": "whisper"}],
  "ending": {"kind": "tap"}
}
```

| Layer | Kinds | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| `background` | `fall-asleep` (default), `black`, `fade-to-black`, `none` | `seconds` 0.5–60, 0 means 5 |
| `overlays` (On top) | `eye` (default colour `#3dff7a`), `embers` (`#ff9a3c`) | at most 8; `at` 0–120; `seconds` 0.5–60; `params.color` `#rrggbb` |
| `text` (Words) | style `plain` (default), `whisper`, `title` | at most 12 lines; `say` 1–280 characters, control characters dropped; `seconds` 0 or less means to the end |
| `ending` | `tap` (default), `auto` | `after` (auto only) is seconds after the last layer finishes, 0–60, default 3 |

**Looks** (`params.look`) choose how a kind with several ways of being drawn
is drawn. `Looks` in `model.go` mirrors the engine's `ChronicleCutscene.looks`
table, ids, plain names and order alike; the first is the engine's default
(`TestEngineLooksMatchTheServer` reads the engine to keep them equal).

| Kind | Looks (first is the default) |
|---|---|
| `fall-asleep` background | `ink` Ink in water, `eyelids` Heavy eyelids, `drift` The page drifts off |
| `eye` overlay | `rift` Burning seam, `almond` Lids part, `gather` Sparks gather |

`Normalize` drops a look that is unknown or on a kind without looks, so the
engine plays the default; the AI block is refused by name instead (see AI).

**Pupils** (`params.pupil`, eye only) are `round` Round or `slit` Dragon slit
(a tall reptilian slit in a scaled, veined iris), any look. Left empty, the eye
draws its look's own (`Look.Pupil`: round for `rift`, slit for `almond` and
`gather`), so the approved ink and rift default keeps its round pupil.
`Pupils` mirrors the engine's `ChronicleCutscene.pupils` and each eye look's
`pupil` (`TestEnginePupilsMatchTheServer`). `Normalize` drops an unknown pupil
or one on anything but an eye.

Numbers out of range are clamped, not refused, and rounded to 0.1 s; a word
that is not a number, an unknown kind or a bad colour is refused by name. Names
are unique per campaign, ignoring case, so the AI can find a scene by name.

## A showing

Showing a scene stores a **snapshot** of it in `cutscene_showings` (editing or
deleting the scene later never changes what was shown), with one row per
chosen person in `cutscene_showing_viewers`. A viewer may play it again until
`rewatch_until` (1 hour, 12 hours (the default, "Tonight") or 3 days); the
Director may read any showing at any time. Anyone else, and a viewer after the
window, gets the same 404 as a showing that does not exist. A showing reads as
live (the editor lists it as showing) until it is stopped or 5 minutes have
passed; Stop stays on the "Shown lately" list until it is stopped or its window
closes, since a scene that waits for a tap can stay up longer. Stopping is
idempotent and does not shorten the rewatch window: a stopped showing never
plays by itself when a player opens Chronicle (`stopped` on `/mine`), but they
can still watch it again.

## Who may do what

The route group carries `RequireAuth`, `RequireCampaignAccess`,
`RequireRole(RolePlayer)` and `RequireAddon("cutscenes")`. The Director is
`cc.CanAuthorDmOnly()` (owner or DM-granted); every Director route answers 404
"page not found" to anyone else, checked in the handler before any form is
read and again in the service.

| Route | Who | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /campaigns/:id/cutscenes` | Director | list page |
| `GET /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/new` | Director | editor with the eye scene |
| `POST /campaigns/:id/cutscenes` | Director | create, redirect to the editor |
| `POST /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/preview` | Director | the normalised scene JSON of the posted form, unsaved |
| `POST /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/preview/foundry` | Director, 30/min | plays the posted form, checked as Preview checks it, on the Director's own Foundry screen; `{sent: true}`, or 409 with the reason (campaign not using Foundry, or the module not connected now). Nothing stored |
| `GET /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/showings` | Director | recent showings (HTMX fragment, else `{data,total}` of `ShowingReport`) |
| `POST /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/showings/:shid/stop` | Director | the stopped row (HTMX; `?from=recent` answers the "Shown lately" row, kept whole and marked stopped) or `{showingId, stopped: true}` |
| `GET` / `POST /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/:sid` | Director | editor / save |
| `POST /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/:sid/delete` | Director | delete, redirect to the list |
| `GET /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/:sid/show` | Director | the Show sheet fragment |
| `POST /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/:sid/show` | Director, 30/min | `to` (repeated user ids), `rewatch` (`1h`/`12h`/`72h`); answers the live row |
| `GET /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/mine` | any member | `{data,total}` of `{showingId,name,shownAt,rewatchUntil,watched,stopped}`, newest first, only that member's, only inside the window |
| `GET /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/showings/:shid` | viewer in window, or Director | `{showingId,rewatchUntil,scene}` |
| `POST /campaigns/:id/cutscenes/showings/:shid/watched` | viewer, 120/min | 204; marks the first watch only |

The sync API adds `GET /api/v1/campaigns/:id/cutscenes/showings?user=<id>`
(`syncapi.CutsceneAPIHandler`): an owner or co-DM key, `PermRead`, add-on
gated; answers `{data,total}` of `{showingId,name,shownAt,rewatchUntil,scene}`
for that member's showings still inside their window. Foundry reads it when a
player opens the Cut scenes tab of their Satchel, for their watch-again list.

## Live messages

Published by `cutsceneLivePublisher` in `internal/app/cutscenes_adapters.go`
after the database write. `Message.Sources` keeps each copy on its own kind of
socket, so a player with Chronicle and Foundry both open is not shown the scene
twice by the hub.

| Type | Payload | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| `cutscene.show` | `{showingId, scene}` | the chosen people (`StrictAudience`), browser sockets only |
| `cutscene.stop` | `{showingId}` | same |
| `cutscene.foundry` | `{showingId, viewers, scene, rewatchUntil}` | DM-held sockets of the Foundry module only; the module plays it for the listed users |
| `cutscene.foundry_stop` | `{showingId}` | same |
| `cutscene.foundry_preview` | `{previewId, director, scene}` | same; the module plays it on the GM screen of `director` if matched and playing, else the active GM's, and relays it to no other client |

None is a change-feed type: a showing missed while offline is found through
`/cutscenes/mine` (browser) or the sync API (Foundry), not replayed.

## Presence

The Show sheet says where each member is: In Chronicle, In Foundry, Both or
Away (`cutscenePresenceAdapter`). It reads the same signals as the DM Screen's
"here" list (`presenceSignals` in `dm_screen_adapters.go`) but keeps the two
places apart. "Everyone here" ticks every player who is anywhere but Away; it
leaves Directors out, the asker included, since a Director's screens (and
their Foundry screen, the GM's) run the table. A Director ticks themselves by
hand to see a showing. "Nobody else is in this campaign yet." shows when the
asker is the only one listed.

## The Director's pages

`list_page.templ`, `editor_page.templ`, `show_sheet.templ`; styles in
`static/css/cutscenes.css`; the `cutscene-workshop` widget
(`static/js/cutscene_workshop.js`) holds only Preview, which posts the form to
`/preview` and loads the engine lazily from `data-engine-src`. It plays the
scene over the whole window exactly as the player stage does (the same
`play(document.body, …)` call, never `contained`), so the Director sees what a
player sees, the page dimming from its edges included. **Preview in Foundry**
shows beside it when the campaign's Sync API add-on is on (making a Foundry key
turns it on; `cutsceneFoundryPreview` in `internal/app`): it posts the same
form to `/preview/foundry` and says "Sent to your Foundry screen" in
`data-csw-sent`, or the refusal in the error line. Background and an eye row
each have a Look picker, shown only for the kind that has looks, and an eye row
a Pupil picker (`ov_pupil`: the look's own, Round or Dragon slit). Every control in
an HTMX fragment is an inline IIFE built in `pages.go`. The editor's sections
are Background, On top, Words and Ending; list rows are repeated positional
fields (`ov_kind`, `ov_at`, …, `tx_say`, …) and blank spare rows are skipped
(`form.go`).

## AI

`ai.go` (kind `cut-scene`) and `ai_lookup.go` (lookup `cut-scenes`, tool
`cut_scenes`, owner-only). Director keys only, add-on on only. The AI may
create, update and delete through review; delete carries the reviewed scene's
hash and refuses a scene changed since. It never shows a scene: `show_to`,
`players`, `rewatch` and their kin are refused by name. An update changes only
the layers it writes, and a list replaces the whole list. `rename_to` renames.
`look:` sits inside `background` (fall-asleep only) and an eye overlay (also
read from `params.look`); an unknown look, or one on a kind without looks, is
refused by name, and the review names the look in words, saying "(the
default)" when none was written. `pupil:` sits on an eye overlay (also read
from `params.pupil`); an unknown one, or one on embers or the background, is
refused by name, and the review names it, saying "(the look's own)" when none
was written. The block format is in `Doc()`; the lookup
answers each scene as a block that can be pasted back unchanged, and lists
every look and pupil with one plain line.
