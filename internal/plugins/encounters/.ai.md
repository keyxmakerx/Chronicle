# Encounters Plugin

## Purpose

Fights a DM plans on a page and loads in Foundry on game night. An encounter
belongs to one page (an Adventures trial, a location, any page). It names the
heroes it was built for and one row per creature: the creature's page, how
many, its initiative group (A to H) and how it starts the fight: **seen**,
**hidden** (placed with its token hidden) or **later** (left off the scene
until the DM places it). Nothing is revealed automatically; Foundry's
Encounters tab shows or hides a creature only when the DM presses it.

Everything here is the DM's. The service refuses any viewer below Owner
visibility on every method, the block draws only for the DM, and the sync API
routes need the owner or a co-DM, so a planned ambush never reaches a player.

## The block and the builder

`page_encounters` is a layout block (template context, sizes 1x2, 2x2, 4x2),
always registered because the Adventures layout places it
(`tile-adv-encounters`). The widget lists the page's encounters; pressing one
slides the builder out from the right: name, hero tick boxes (pages in
character categories), creature rows (pages in creature categories, or a
name with no page), Ready for game night, and Delete. It saves as you go
(600ms after the last change), one save at a time carrying the version, and
reloads on a conflict.

A game system adds its own panel through `chronicle:encounter-draft`, fired
on `document` after every change with `{campaignId, encounter: {id, name,
heroes, creatures}, slot}`; the system fills `slot` (Draw Steel draws its
encounter budget there). The builder never computes a budget itself.

## Table

`encounters (id PK, campaign_id, entity_id, name, ready, data LONGTEXT,
version, created_by, updated_by, created_at, updated_at)`. FKs to core
`entities` and `campaigns` cascade, so deleting a page removes its
encounters; a trashed page's encounters stay with it and are left out of the
Foundry list until it is restored.

## Routes (JSON)

| Method | Path | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| GET | `/campaigns/:id/pages/:eid/encounters` | `{encounters, canEdit}` |
| POST | `/campaigns/:id/pages/:eid/encounters` | body `{name, ready, heroes, creatures}`; 50 per page |
| GET | `/campaigns/:id/encounters/:encid` | one encounter |
| PUT | `/campaigns/:id/encounters/:encid` | whole encounter plus `version`; mismatch is 409 |
| DELETE | `/campaigns/:id/encounters/:encid` | 204 |
| GET | `/api/v1/campaigns/:id/encounters?q=&limit=` | Foundry: `{data, total}`, ready first |
| GET | `/api/v1/campaigns/:id/encounters/:encounterID` | Foundry: one encounter |

## Rules

- **Who**: Owner visibility (the owner, or a member given DM access) on every
  method; editing also needs the page's own edit rule. A page that is hidden
  or in another campaign is 404 before anything else.
- **Pages named in an encounter** (heroes, creature rows) must be live pages
  of the campaign the DM may see, checked on every save. On read, a hero
  whose page has gone is dropped and a creature row whose page has gone is
  unlinked (its name stays), so Foundry falls back to the name.
- **Limits**: name 120 characters, 12 heroes, 30 creature rows, 1 to 40 of
  each, 50 encounters per page.
