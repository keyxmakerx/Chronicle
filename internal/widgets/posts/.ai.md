# Posts Widget

Named content sections attached to an entity, shown below its main entry. Each
post has its own TipTap-edited body and an optional privacy flag; the main
`entry`/`entry_html` stays the overview, posts are per-section deep-dives (e.g.
"Background", "DM Secrets").

## Files
- `model.go` — Post + DTO types; includes `EntryHTML` + `IsPrivate` fields
- `repository.go` — SQL CRUD against the `posts` table
- `service.go` — business logic (HTML sanitization on write via `sanitize.HTML`)
- `secrets.go` — `stripPostSecrets` removes GM-only text, pictures and rollers from the body of every post read by anyone but the owner and Co-DMs; hiding private posts alone is not enough
- `handler.go` — HTTP handlers (HTMX fragments + JSON)
- `routes.go` — `RegisterRoutes(e, h, campaignSvc, authSvc)` mounts everything under `/campaigns/:id/entities/:eid/posts`
- `static/js/widgets/entity_posts.js` — widget UI (JS-mounted, no `.templ`)

## Routes (under `/campaigns/:id/entities/:eid/posts`)
- `GET /` — list posts (privacy-filtered for caller); readable on public campaigns without a login (OptionalAuth + public-campaign access)
- `POST /` — create post (Scribe)
- `PUT /reorder` — reorder posts (Scribe)
- `PUT /:pid` — edit post (Scribe)
- `DELETE /:pid` — delete post (Scribe)

A Scribe sees what players see (`requirePostWrite` in `secrets.go`): a GM-only
post reads as missing to them, they cannot make a post GM-only, and they cannot
change or delete the text of a post with GM-only parts, because their copy of it
left those parts out. Renaming or reordering such a post is allowed. The owner
and Co-DMs (`CanAuthorDmOnly`) are not limited.

Constructed inline in `internal/app/routes.go`, registered via `posts.RegisterRoutes(...)`; session-cookie auth plus campaign-access gate.

Dependencies: `entities` (context), `campaigns` (access), `auth`, `sanitize` (every write).

Export/import: `postExportAdapter` and `postImportAdapter` in `internal/app/`
join the campaigns export; the round-trip is lossless, with no re-sanitization on egress.
